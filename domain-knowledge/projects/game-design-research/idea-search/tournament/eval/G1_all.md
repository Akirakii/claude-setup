### G101 Lone Scout
- Rule (one sentence): The fake floor tiles show as holes only on the screen of a player with no friend within 15 m, and one player may secretly want the crew to fail.
- Hub (the one shared thing everyone uses): the temple floor, generated each run, where fake tiles look solid to anyone near a friend. A player alone slowly freezes, so scouts must come back.
- Coupling (how my best move depends on what a friend just did; who does which different action): The group can't see the fake tiles while together, so someone steps away to see them and calls them out. The group picks the route while the scout warns, and the scout must stand where the group is heading. As the cold drains the scout, another player takes over.
- Fantasy (who you are, what you're doing): tomb raiders in a temple whose traps reveal themselves only to someone alone in the dark.
- Look (one line): a torch-lit stone hall, the group in warm light, the scout in cold blue light where the holes glow.
- At 3 / at 6: At 3 there is one scout and two walkers, and a traitor appears only in some runs, since one traitor is a third of the crew. At 6 two scouts can stand on different sides and check each other's calls.
- Hidden (what, how it's uncovered, why anyone hides it) or "none": which tiles are fake (uncovered by going alone, or by throwing a torch onto a tile); who the traitor is (uncovered by sending a second scout and comparing calls). The traitor hides because they win if the crew falls short.
- A failed run (what happens, who is blamed): Tom calls "third tile on the left is safe" and Mia drops through it. Tom is blamed in the moment; whether it was a lie or a mistake is the argument after.

### G109 Thin Floors

- Hidden (what, how it's uncovered, why anyone hides it) or "none": each room is seen only by the player in it: which shelves hold what, and whether the hatch is blocked. Others uncover it by asking over voice. Nobody gains by hiding it.
