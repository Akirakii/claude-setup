# Friend-group party games: who succeeded, who failed, and the shared formula

19 online party games for a group of friends (co-op, social deduction, 1-vs-many, brawlers), each with its numbers and why it worked or died.
Tags: [source] = said by the linked source; [read] = Claude's reading across several sources; [opinion] = Claude's view; (memory) = from Claude's memory, not searched. Numbers are third-party estimates unless the developer said them.

## The problem in general terms
A short online party game for a group of friends, sold cheaply on Steam, where each round turns one player into the
threat for everyone else. What makes these games sell, last, and get streamed, and what kills them?

## 1. The games

### Co-op "friendslop" (friends on one side, against the game)

**Lethal Company** (2023, solo dev Zeekerss). 4-player co-op scavenging on monster-filled moons, proximity voice.
- Success: all-time peak 240,817 concurrent on Steam, 3 Dec 2023 [source: [SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/app/1966720/charts)];
  98% positive from ~150k reviews, 5.4M copies (~$43M gross) by Dec 2023 [source: [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/editorial-lethal-company-the-indie)];
  later estimates 10M+ copies [source: [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/lethal-company-sold-an-estimated-10-million-copies)]. Price $9.99 (memory).
- Why: scary + funny + absurd mix that suits players and streamers; peak 190k viewers on Twitch; random rooms so each
  run differs; early players acted as marketers [source: GameDev Reports, above].

**R.E.P.O.** (2025, semiwork). Up to 6 robots drag fragile loot out of monster mansions with physics, proximity voice.
- Success: peak 271,571 concurrent, 23 Mar 2025 [source: [SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/app/3241660/charts)]; 18.5M copies on
  Steam in 2025 [source: [AppMagic via GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-friendslop-games-in-2025)];
  ~96–97% positive [source: [SteamPulse](https://steampulse.org/game/3241660)]. Made as a "let's fail quickly" project
  [source: [Gamepressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/newsroom/lets-just-fail-quickly-how-a-game-meant-to-flop-became-a-steam-hi/z07dad)].

**Peak** (2025, Aggro Crab + Landfall). 4-player co-op climbing, built from a game jam.
- Success: 10M copies in about two months, peak ~171k concurrent [source: [AOL/Alinea](https://www.aol.com/peak-sells-10-million-copies-155741375.html)];
  15.4M copies on Steam in 2025 [source: AppMagic, above]. A $4.95 sale drove the late-summer surge [source: AOL, above].

**Content Warning** (2024, Landfall). Film your friends being killed by monsters, upload the video for views.
- Success: free for 24 h on launch, 6.2M+ claims [source: [GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/a-24-hour-free-giveaway-sent-steams-new-viral-co-op-mega-hit-to-the-moon-with-62-million-owners-after-its-first-day)];
  peak 204,439 concurrent [source: [SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/app/2881650/charts)]; still 2.2M sold by June 2024
  [source: [robotsoverdinosaurs](https://robotsoverdinosaurs.net/?p=53101)]. D30 retention ~3% [source: AppMagic, above].

**Phasmophobia** (2020, Kinetic Games). 1–4 player ghost hunting, the game listens to your voice.
- Success: 25M+ copies across platforms [source: [KitGuru](https://www.kitguru.net/tech-news/mustafa-mahmoud/phasmophobia-hits-new-sales-milestone-with-over-25-million-copies-sold)];
  D30 retention 5.3%, called the forerunner of the genre [source: AppMagic, above].
- Why: co-op + proximity chat + unpredictable reactions made play a shared performance [source: [AppMagic blog](https://appmagic.rocks/blog/friendslop-steam-games-2025)].

### Social deduction / betrayal

**Among Us** (2018, blew up 2020). 4–15 players, crewmates do tasks, impostors kill, meetings vote.
- Success: Steam peak 438,524 (Sep 2020); 3.8M concurrent across PC + mobile; a 75% Summer Sale discount, then
  Brazilian and Korean streamers, then English streamers in July 2020 drove it [source: [Global Games Forum](https://globalgamesforum.com/features/the-long-and-winding-road-to-success-for-among-us), [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/news/among-us-hits-100-million-downloads-3-8-million-concurrent-players)];
  later ~31.6k monthly Steam peak [source: [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/among-us/the-quick-rise-even-quicker-fall-among-us-what-really-happened)].

**Goose Goose Duck** (2021). Free Among Us-like with proximity voice.
- Success: peak 563,677 concurrent after a BTS stream spike [source: [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/news/goose-goose-duck-game-steam-charts)];
  still ~4k monthly average in 2026 [source: [GameCharts](https://gamecharts.org/steam/goose-goose-duck)].

**Deceit / Deceit 2** (2017 / 2023). Two infected hide among humans, then turn into monsters; Deceit 2 added a werewolf Terror.
- Success (Deceit 2): peak 731 concurrent, "mostly negative" mainly over the tiny player base and long queues; went
  free-to-play to fix it [source: [Vaporlens](https://vaporlens.app/app/2064870/deceit_2), [GameWatcher](https://gamewatcher.com/news/deceit-2-goes-free-to-play-releasing-on-consoles-in-april)].

**Project Winter** (2019). 8 survivors, hidden traitors, snowy survival.
- Success: ~335K copies, peak listed 1,623 [source: [Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/774861/Project_Winter)].

**Dread Hunger** (2022). 8 explorers, 2 hidden thralls, survival ship.
- Success then death: daily peaks fell to ~5,000, not enough to fund development; servers kept getting DDoSed; delisted
  Dec 2023, shut Jan 2024 [source: [Gamepressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/newsroom/2-years-ago-dread-hunger-was-a-steam-hit-now-its-about-to-die/z36463), [Delisted Games](https://delistedgames.com/?p=23607)].
- [read] Project Winter and Dread Hunger were long (20–30 min rounds, memory), needed 8 strangers, and ran on servers; all three hurt them.

### Asymmetric 1-vs-many

**Dead by Daylight** (2016, Behaviour). 1 killer vs 4 survivors.
- Success: 60M+ copies against a 300k forecast; ~70M players; 2025 its best year; record 125,820 Steam concurrent a decade in
  [source: [FRVR](https://frvr.com/blog/news/dead-by-daylight-is-now-the-most-played-horror-game-in-history-with-2026-primed-to-be-the-games-biggest-year-ever), [Final Boss](https://finalboss.io/dead-by-daylight-is-chasing-a-billion-players)];
  D30 retention 11.3%, ~4x friendslop [source: AppMagic, above].
- Weakness: asymmetry makes matchmaking hard to balance; killer vs survivor queue times swing [source: [ComicBook](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/dead-by-daylight-players-not-happy-new-matchmaking/)].
- Why it lasts [read]: horror licences and a steady stream of new killers; a live team for ten years. Out of reach for a solo developer.

**Evolve** (2015, Turtle Rock / 2K). 4 hunters vs 1 monster.
- Failure: Steam peak just over 27k in month one, under 7k the next month [source: [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/uk/what-evolves-monster-taught-me-about-failings-4v1-multiplayer)].
- Why, per its designer: "We never really solved 4v1"; the two sides shared no systems (the monster fed and levelled,
  hunters didn't), so every new thing was costly to balance [source: [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/-i-evolve-i-dev-4v1-gameplay-caused-more-problems-that-we-ever-imagined-)].

**Friday the 13th: The Game** (2017). 1 Jason vs 7 counsellors.
- End: dedicated servers retired Nov 2020, off sale Dec 2023 when the licence expired, offline end of 2024
  [source: [Delisted Games](https://delistedgames.com/?p=22006), [Pure Xbox](https://purexbox.com/news/2025/01/friday-the-13th-game-now-officially-offline-a-year-after-xbox-store-delisting)].

**Secret Neighbor** (2019, tinyBuild). Kids search a house for keys; one is secretly the Neighbor.
- Success: ~430k–1.3M copies, 86–87% positive, a few hundred concurrent today [source: [Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/859570/Secret_Neighbor_Hello_Neighbor_Multiplayer), [Steambase](https://steambase.io/apps/859570)].

**Murder Mystery 2** (Roblox). 1 murderer, 1 sheriff, the rest innocents; short rounds in small maps.
- Success: ~32 billion visits, 12 years old, 400k+ concurrent most days, peak 1.1–1.4M in summer 2026
  [source: [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/how-nikilis-keeps-murder-mystery-2-alive-after-12-years-and-24-6-billion-visits), [StudioKrew](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-july-2026/)].

### Physics party brawlers / FFA

**Fall Guys** (2020). 60-player obstacle elimination.
- Rise and fall: ~172k Steam peak Aug 2020, average halved in September and fell 60% more in October; free-to-play in
  2022 brought 20M players in 48 h, then faded again [source: [GameRevolution](https://www.gamerevolution.com/?p=709783), [Shacknews](https://shacknews.com/article/131111/fall-guys-free-to-play-20-million-players)].

**Party Animals** (2023). Physics brawler of cute animals, up to 8.
- Success: demo peak 135k (2020), launch peak 104,174 (Sep 2023) but "mostly negative" at launch over servers and no
  offline mode [source: [SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/app/1260320/charts), [GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/co-op-hit-party-animals-already-has-over-100k-steam-players-but-mostly-negative-reviews-as-players-cite-server-problems-and-no-offline-mode)]; ~2.6M copies est. [source: [Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/1260320/Party_Animals)].

**Gang Beasts** (2017). Wobbly physics brawler.
- Success: ~1.3M Steam copies, ~$15M gross est., 86% positive [source: [Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/285900/Gang_Beasts)].

**Human: Fall Flat** (2016). Wobbly co-op physics puzzles.
- Success: 40M+ copies [source: [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/press-release/human-fall-flat-sales-surpasses-a-massive-40-million-worldwide)].
- [read] The physics brawler that went co-op sold ~30x the ones that stayed versus.

Not found: Specimen 8 (no Steam page under that name); Pummel Party (no sales figures found).

## 3. The shared formula

What the hits have in common:
1. **Friends you already have, not strangers.** Every failure found (Deceit 2, Dread Hunger, Project Winter, Evolve) ran
   out of strangers to fill lobbies; the 2023–25 hits are played in your own friend group [read].
2. **Proximity voice as the comedy engine.** It turns play into a shared performance and fills streams [source: AppMagic blog; GameBusiness.jp].
3. **A funny premise over polish.** Players "don't care" about collision or controls if the premise is funny
   [source: [How To Market A Game](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/07/30/is-friendslop-saturated)]; R.E.P.O. and Peak were quick, cheap builds [source].
4. **Cheap: $5–20,** and each friend buys a copy, so a group buy multiplies revenue [source: AppMagic, above].
5. **Streamers and clips are the marketing.** Lethal Company (190k viewers), Among Us, Goose Goose Duck, Chained Together [source].
6. **Waves, not retention.** Friendslop D30 retention is ~3%; a top title takes over 50% of the genre's players, then
   fades to 10% within 3–9 months; this is the business model, not a failure [source: AppMagic; How To Market A Game].
   Sales and updates restart waves (Peak's $4.95 sale, Fall Guys going free) [source].
7. **Peer-to-peer hosting.** Dread Hunger died partly from server costs and DDoS; Friday the 13th lost its servers
   [source]. Hits like Lethal Company host on a friend's machine (memory).

What the failures lacked: a friend group (they needed matchmaking), short rounds, and a cheap way to run online.
Evolve also shows deep asymmetry costs too much to balance [source].
