## 1. Dragon's Hoard, version 1 (failed)


**After critic round 1: failed as written.** Standing still until the eye closes beat every other move, a thrown goblet drew the fire away, the best move was to stop talking, and the order left no room for "one more". Version 2 follows this section.
**Fantasy:** we are burglars in a sleeping dragon's hoard, and every sound we make, our real voices included, stirs it.

**Feelings, and the games that already cause them**
| Feeling | Games that cause it | How they do it | How it fails there | A shipped game causes it? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Held breath: I have to stay quiet and I can barely manage | Lethal Company's eyeless dogs; Phasmophobia; Dig, Dig, Die | the monster reacts to the player's real voice chat, walkie-talkie included | background noise from a bad mic sets the dogs off; one jam reviewer said the silliness took away from the horror | ✅ |
| Greed: one more goblet | Clank! (board game); Lethal Company | loot and movement add noise that makes the dragon more likely to hit you, and deeper loot is better; a quota that rises every cycle with no ceiling | none found for Clank!; for the quota, being behind forces risky runs | ✅ |
| "Who did that?!": blame and laughing | R.E.P.O. | physics loot that shatters on a wall or spills from a cart when a friend bumps it; reviewers put the humour in shared failure | not found | ✅ |
| The scramble when it wakes | Lethal Company | "every action has upsides and downsides", and the crew wants to stick together while the systems push them to split | not found | ✅ |

Sources: [GameRevolution on monsters hearing the mic](https://www.gamerevolution.com/guides/956348-lethal-company-can-monster-hear-talking-speaking-mic), [Prima Games on eyeless dogs](https://primagames.com/tips/how-to-deal-with-eyeless-dog-in-lethal-company), [Voicebot on Phasmophobia](https://voicebot.ai/2021/01/13/speak-too-loudly-while-playing-phasmophobia-and-video-game-ghosts-will-hunt-you-down/), [Dig, Dig, Die on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4074010/), [Clank! review, board-game.co.uk](https://board-game.co.uk/?p=117606), [Clank! on the ICv2 chart](https://icv2.com/articles/news/view/38234/in-space-no-one-can-hear-you-clank), [The real horror of Lethal Company](https://returntosender.neocities.org/posts/2024-02-11-The-Real-Horror-Of-Lethal-Company), [PC Gamer on Lethal Company](https://pcgamer.com/your-new-co-op-survival-horror-obsession-is-about-being-underpaid-and-overworked-space-scavengers), [R.E.P.O., Neon Music](https://neonmusic.co.uk/r-e-p-o-is-the-co-op-horror-game-turning-looting-into-ludicrous-art).

**Job:** fill tonight's order from the buyer (two or three named big pieces and a sum in small loot) and get it to the cart.

**The choice:** carry the big piece with a friend, which means talking each other through the piles, or pocket what I can reach alone in silence.

**Why the two sides both cost:** small loot alone can't fill the order, so the debt grows; a big piece dragged by one burglar is the loudest thing in the cave, and carried by two it needs words ("tilt it", "stop", "your left"), and words are noise.

**The dragon, one rule:** noise fills its wakefulness and quiet drains it slowly; the eye opens as it fills, and when it is full the dragon breathes fire at the spot of the last loud noise, then settles into a lighter sleep than before.
Every noise shows a ring on the floor where it came from, so everyone sees whose it was.

**Scene, 4 friends (what each gains and loses)**
- Ana and Ben lift the crown from the far pile; Cy pockets coins alone on the near pile; Dee waits at the cart with the sack.
- Ana and Ben whisper; each word puts a small ring at their feet, and the eye opens a slit.
- Ben steps on a goblet: a big ring, the eye opens halfway, everyone freezes.
- Ana laughs into her mic. Her ring is the last loud one, so the fire goes to her and Ben; they drop the crown, which clangs, and dive behind the pile.
- Ana and Ben gain the order's best piece for the whole crew and risk themselves; Cy gains safe coins and leaves the order short, so the debt grows for everyone.
- Next night: "Cy never carries" and "Ana laughed" are teasing, and nobody chose to hurt anyone, so no grudge.

**The plan a crew settles into by night 5, and what breaks it:** "pairs carry big pieces on the quiet route with as few words as possible".
That is skill growing, and the order, the layout of the piles and how lightly the dragon sleeps are dealt new each night, so the quiet route moves.
"Take only small loot" fails the order; "nobody speaks at all" fails the two-person carries.

**Between nights:** the buyer's order grows, the debt carries over, and the dragon sleeps lighter each night the crew comes back; pay buys soft shoes, a padded sack, a longer rope.

**With 1 human + bots:** steps, drops and carries make noise without any voice, so "who did that" and the scramble work with bots; the voice part needs friends.

**Marks:** held breath ✅ · greed ✅ · blame ✅ · scramble ✅ (all on paper).

**Unproven**
- Whether whispering stays tense after five nights or turns into silent routine.
- Players who switch their mic off in the game lose the tension; a muted burglar could hum by themselves (Claude).
- Mic background noise, the documented failure of the dogs.

**Near games:** Dig, Dig, Die (graveyards, monsters hear voice chat, released); Treasure Cove (island, noise attracts monsters, not released).
No co-op game about a sleeping dragon's hoard was found.

**A variant not played out (Claude):** with five or six friends, one of them plays the dragon with a black screen that shows only the noise rings, and breathes fire at them.
A person on the other side removes the need for the dragon to get harder by rule, which is what the verbs report says keeps hide-and-seek fresh.
Echophobia does this as one blind hunter against three runners (screaming into the mic lights you up) and is not released: [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4248140).
It turns the game into friends against a friend, which the user questioned on Moonsick's race.

## 1b. Dragon's Hoard, version 2 (rewritten after critic round 1; failed round 2)

**After critic round 2: dropped.** Whoever the eye can burn can also see the eye, so the friend who needs the call doesn't exist. Freezing at every noise ring wins without a word. Where the eye looks was never written, and each way of reading it has a one-line answer. "Voices only count while the eye is open" contradicts the fantasy line, and on Discord the call is free. The cave mouth resets the smoke and the dragon together. With one burglar left, nothing can be carried. Two rounds of fixes, six severe findings after four: the critic says not to write a version 3, and Claude agrees.

**Fantasy (unchanged):** we are burglars in a sleeping dragon's hoard, and every sound we make, our real voices included, stirs it.

**What changed, and which finding each change answers**
| Finding (severe) | Change |
|---|---|
| No clock: standing still until the eye closes wins | The cave is full of the dragon's smoke, and a burglar coughs more the longer they stay inside. A cough is noise. Past a couple of minutes a burglar's own coughing fills the dragon's wakefulness faster than quiet drains it, so waiting inside makes things worse. Fresh air at the cave mouth clears it. |
| The fire goes to the last loud noise, so a thrown goblet draws it away | Noise only decides when the eye opens. The eye then looks along the cave for a few seconds and burns what it sees moving or standing in the open. A thrown goblet only wakes it sooner. |
| The core is built on silence, and the best move is to stop talking | Voices only count while the eye is open. The rest of the time the crew talks freely. While the eye is open, the friend who can see where it is looking holds what the friend in the open needs to know. |
| Greed has no rule | Loot beyond the order pays down the debt and buys gear, so "one more" is something the crew can argue about against the smoke in their lungs. (Lethal Company lets a crew bank scrap for a later quota: memory, not searched.) |
| Idle seats (the cart holder; four watching one pair at 6) | No cart seat. The order is one big piece for every two burglars, and dawn ends the night. This holds only if the numbers are set so that one pair at a time can't fill the order before dawn. |

**Feelings, with the row the critic struck**
| Feeling | Games that cause it, with the same relation between players | A shipped game causes it? |
|---|---|---|
| Held breath | Lethal Company's eyeless dogs and Phasmophobia (a co-op crew, a monster that hears the mic) | ✅ |
| "Who did that?!" | R.E.P.O. (a co-op crew, physics loot) | ✅ |
| The scramble when it wakes | Lethal Company | ✅ |
| Greed: one more goblet | Lethal Company's rising quota. Clank! is struck: its players are rivals, so the extra goblet beats someone. | ✅ for the quota; the banking rule is memory |

**The dragon, one rule:** noise fills its wakefulness and quiet drains it.
At half, the eye opens and looks along one line of the cave for a few seconds; it puffs fire at what it sees moving or standing in the open.
At full, it rears and sweeps the whole floor; everyone not behind a pile is charred; then it sleeps lighter than before.
A charred burglar is a statue until two friends carry them to the cave mouth; a statue's voice is cut off.

**The choice:** the eye is open and turning toward my friend, who can't see it from behind the pile. Call out, and add my voice to what it hears, or stay silent and hope they keep still.

**Why both sides cost:** if the wakefulness is low, a call costs little and saves the friend; if it is nearly full, the call tips the dragon into the full sweep, which chars the friend anyway and everyone else in the open.
Where the wakefulness stands, who is in the open and who can see the eye are different every time.

**Scene, 3 friends (the fewest)**
- Ana and Ben carry the crown across the open floor; Cy pockets coins on a pile near the head and is starting to cough.
- Cy coughs; the eye opens, and from where he stands he sees it start to turn toward the crown.
- Ana and Ben, backs to the head, see only the ring from the cough.
- Cy calls "freeze"; they stop, the crown held between them; the eye passes over two still shapes and closes.
- Cy gains nothing for himself and raised the wakefulness for everyone; Ana and Ben keep the crown.
- If the wakefulness had been near full, the same call would have charred all three, and "why did you shout" is next night's teasing.

**Scene, 6 friends:** three pairs carry at once, and three sets of lungs cough; stirs come often, and at each one the friends near the head hold the call for the pairs in the open. Nobody waits outside, because the order needs all three pairs before dawn.

**With 1 human + bots:** a "psst" key makes the same noise as a short call, and bots freeze when they hear it, so the choice is the same without a voice; steps, drops and coughs need no voice at all.

**The plan by night 5, and what the rules answer**
- "Stand still until the eye closes": the smoke makes waiting inside worse.
- "Throw a goblet into a far corner": it only adds noise.
- "Nobody speaks": voices are free while the eye is closed, and during a stir the call is the only way the friend in the open learns where the eye is looking.
- "Always carry along the piles, never in the open": the piles and the big pieces are placed anew each night, and some pieces sit in the open.
- Not answered on paper: a crew may learn to freeze at every ring without being told, and then the call is never needed.

**Older checks, run before the marks (the step the critic found missing)**
| Check | Pass? |
|---|---|
| Something gets worse when nobody acts | ✅ the smoke |
| A decoy noise beats the threat | ✅ it doesn't; the eye looks, it doesn't listen for a place |
| The threat hunts whoever did the last thing | ✅ it doesn't |
| The goal is a place to reach | ✅ it isn't; the order is |
| Works at 3 and at 6 with nobody idle | ❌ at 6 only if the numbers force three pairs |
| The core act doesn't need two players to move in the same half second | ❌ the two-person carry does |
| The same relation between players as in the cited games | ✅ after striking Clank! |
| A drop reason from this file applies | ✅ none found: no decoy, no shared waterline with open facts, no shipped Steam game with the premise (tabletop and itch.io games have it) |

**Marks:** held breath ✅ · blame ✅ · scramble ✅ · greed ✅ (on paper, version 2).

**Still failing or open**
- The two-person carry over the network (the lag check fails; R.E.P.O. ships it, which shows it can be done and not that it is cheap).
- Six players depend on the numbers.
- Whether a crew still needs the call by night 20.

## 2. Low Tide


**After critic round 1: dropped.** Everyone wants the same thing and sees the same water, so "leave or wait" is one sum the crew does together, which is the reason Overloaded was dropped and Claude didn't run it against this idea. The looting is side by side. Mudventure shipped the premise in June 2026. The "agony at the boat" row leaned on Lethal Company's vote by dead players, who have nothing to lose.
**Fantasy:** we are treasure hunters on a wreck that only shows at low tide, the sea is coming back, and the rowboat won't wait forever.

**Feelings, and the games that already cause them**
| Feeling | Games that cause it | How they do it | How it fails there | A shipped game causes it? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Greed: one more room | Lethal Company; Clank!; Deep Sea Adventure | a rising quota; better loot the deeper you go, and you still have to get out | Deep Sea Adventure: once players learn it, games play out the same way, and an early big grab can drown everyone | ✅ |
| "Wait for me!": fear of being left | Lethal Company | the ship's autopilot leaves at midnight with or without you | players complain when it leaves without warning | ✅ |
| The agony of the one at the boat: leave or wait | Lethal Company | the dead vote to make the ship leave early, whether or not anyone is aboard | one dead player can strand the rest; mods raise the votes needed | ✅ |
| Laughing at whoever got left on a rock | Lethal Company | (assumed from the same moments) | not found | ❌ not sourced |

Sources: [Lethal Company early-leave vote, Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1966720/discussions/0/4413046903960344281), [the early-vote mod](https://thunderstore.io/c/lethal-company/p/Azx/Early_Vote_Settings/), [Deep Sea Adventure review, Erik Twice](https://eriktwice.com/en/2019/09/19/deep-sea-adventure-2/), [Deep Sea Adventure review, The Thoughtful Gamer](https://thethoughtfulgamer.com/2018/11/28/deep-sea-adventure-review/), [There Will Be Games](https://therewillbe.games/articles-boardgame-reviews/7767-deep-sea-adventure).

**Job:** strip the wreck of what the harbour master's list asks for and row it back.

**The choice (two friends decide at the same moment):** the one inside goes one room deeper for the listed piece, or turns back now; the one at the boat casts off now and strands whoever is still inside, or waits while the swell fills the boat.

**The sea, one rule:** the water rises at a steady pace all night and the lowest rooms flood first; a boat that waits takes on water from the swell and has to be bailed by hand.

**Scene, 4 friends**
- Ana and Ben are in the hold after the ship's bell on the list; Cy is back at the boat with a small sack; Dee is on deck halfway.
- The water reaches the hold's door; Ben turns back, Ana stays for the bell.
- Cy is bailing; the boat is a third full. Dee shouts "she's coming".
- Cy waits; Ana swims out with the bell, slow because of its weight, and reaches the boat as it is half full; they row back low in the water.
- Ana gains the listed piece for the crew and risks being stranded with it; Cy gains nothing by waiting except the bell, and risks the boat and everything in it.
- Next night: if Cy had left, "Cy left me" is a real complaint, softened because the boat was sinking in plain sight.

**The plan by night 5, and what breaks it:** "everyone out when the water reaches the second deck".
The list, where its pieces lie and the swell are dealt each night, so the same water line is too early one night and too late the next.

**Between nights:** the debt to the harbour carries over and the list grows; each night's tide is lower and uncovers a deeper deck of the same wreck.

**With 1 human + bots:** all physical (carry, swim, bail, cast off), so it works; a bot at the boat needs a rule for when it leaves.

**Marks:** greed ✅ · fear of being left ✅ · the boat's agony ✅ · laughing at the stranded ❌ (no source).

**Unproven**
- It is Lethal Company's loop in one place; whether one wreck holds interest for many nights.
- Deep Sea Adventure's documented problem: the nights may start to play the same.

**Near games:** Mudventure (4-player co-op, caves show at low tide, early access); Raiders of the Lost Island (2020, a sinking island, local play).

## 3. The Glasses


**After critic round 1: dropped.** One seat holds every call (listed here under "Unproven" and sent on anyway). The ghosts only move because someone wears the glasses, so glasses left on the floor in a far room clear the house. The pointing fix for bots gives away the split. It doesn't run at 3 players. In The Blackout Club any player can close their eyes, so it has no fixed seer. ByeByeObake! (Steam page 9 October 2026) has the premise: one who sees the spirits calls them out for one who can't.
**Fantasy:** only whoever wears grandma's glasses can see the ghosts in her house, and the rest of us have to trust that voice.

**Feelings, and the games that already cause them**
| Feeling | Games that cause it | How they do it | How it fails there | A shipped game causes it? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blind trust: I step where I'm told | Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes | one player sees the bomb, the others hold the manual; "at its heart a game about communication"; bombs are generated so nothing can be memorized | none found (200,000 sold by 2016) | ✅ |
| The seer's overload: too many to watch, everyone asking | Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes | a timer and only two mistakes allowed | none found | ✅ |
| "Your left!": miscommunication | The Blackout Club | its monster is only visible with eyes closed, so someone has to stop looking at the world to see it | reviewers said co-op joking lowered the fear; reception was mixed | ✅ (for comedy) |

Sources: [PCGamesN on Keep Talking's sales and design](https://www.pcgamesn.com/keep-talking-and-nobody-explodes/keep-talking-and-nobody-explodes-has-sold-over-200000-units-and-heres-how), [GDC talk listing](https://gdcvault.com/play/1023113/contactUs), [PC Gamer on The Blackout Club](https://www.pcgamer.com/the-blackout-club-stars-a-terrifying-monster-that-wouldnt-work-without-co-op), [DualShockers review](https://www.dualshockers.com/the-blackout-club-review/).

**Job:** carry grandma's heirlooms out of the house before the bank takes it on Friday.

**How the sight is split:** the glasses show the ghosts and turn the house into grey fog, so the wearer can't see walls, furniture or doors and has to be led by the hand; everyone else sees the house and no ghosts.
Wearing them frosts the wearer over, and the ghosts drift toward the glasses.

**The choice (the wearer's):** stay close to the friends carrying the piano so they hear my calls, and bring the ghosts with me, or walk away to pull the ghosts off them, where my voice barely carries.

**The ghosts, one rule:** they stand still until the glasses are near, then drift toward them; touching one frosts that body.

**Scene, 4 friends**
- Ana wears the glasses; Ben holds her hand; Cy and Dee carry the piano.
- Ana: "stop, one right in front of the piano, go around the left". Cy: "our left is the wall". Ana can't see walls.
- Two ghosts drift toward Ana; she walks away to draw them off, and from the next room her calls are too faint to follow.
- Ben, watching the piano, walks Ana into a ghost; her arm frosts.
- Ana gains nothing for herself and takes the frost; Cy and Dee gain the piano for the crew and depend on a voice they can barely hear.
- Next night: "Ben walked me into a ghost" is teasing.

**The plan by night 5, and what breaks it:** "one wearer, one guide, two movers, swap wearers when the frost is half".
The swap needs two bodies standing still together while the ghosts come to the glasses, so when it is safe depends on where the ghosts are.
This is the weakest plan answer of the five ideas.

**Between nights:** five nights until Friday, the list of heirlooms grows, and each night the house holds more ghosts.

**With 1 human + bots:** voice alone fails the user's test crew, so the wearer also points, and a pointed-at ghost shows to everyone for a second (Claude); with that, either seat can be a bot.

**Marks:** blind trust ✅ · the seer's overload ✅ · miscommunication ✅ (on paper).

**Unproven**
- The wearer is close to the one seat that holds every call, a failure the critics have caught five times before.
- Whether being led by the hand is fun for the wearer or only slow.

**Near games:** Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes (the split), The Blackout Club (a monster one player sees).
The idea pool already has a sight-carrying core.

## 4. Echo Bats


**After critic round 1: dropped.** The two feelings that make it co-op have no shipped game. "The owl dives at the most recent squeak" is beaten by one bat under a root squeaking all night, the decoy plan that got Toys dropped. Six bats, or friends chatting, light the whole cave. Flying in the dark is months of feel work.
**Fantasy:** we are young bats, we only see what our squeaks light up, and the owl hears every squeak.

**Feelings, and the games that already cause them**
| Feeling | Games that cause it | How they do it | How it fails there | A shipped game causes it? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lost in the dark, calling for friends | Stifled | sound is how the player sees, and enemies react to the same sound; the mic is optional | single-player only | ✅ |
| Dread: the thing turns toward whoever made the sound | Lethal Company's eyeless dogs; HUSHED | the monster goes after whoever set off its hearing | mic background noise | ✅ |
| Relief when a friend's squeak shows me the way | none found | | | ❌ |
| A laugh that lights up the whole cave | none found | | | ❌ |

Sources: [Stifled on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/514830/Stifled), [Destructoid on Stifled](https://destructoid.com/voice-controlled-horror-game-stifled-is-on-steam-greenlight-worth-a-look), [HUSHED on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4532140/HUSHED/), [Shacknews on Voidwood](https://shacknews.com/article/151048/voidwood-is-an-upcoming-co-op-horror-game-that-uses-your-mic-against-you).

**Job:** catch moths for the colony before dawn; moths only show inside a squeak's ring.

**The choice:** squeak loud enough to light the branches around my friend, and bring the owl onto me, or stay quiet while they fly blind into the thorns.

**The owl, one rule:** it dives at the most recent squeak, so whoever squeaks holds its attention for a few seconds.
A crash makes a sound too, with its own small ring.

**Scene, 4 friends**
- Ana chases a moth into the thorn tree; she has stopped squeaking because the owl is circling.
- Ben, in the open, sees her last ring fade and squeaks hard; the ring crosses the thorn tree and Ana sees the gap.
- The owl turns to Ben; he drops under a root.
- Cy laughs into his mic; the cave lights up, and the owl leaves Ben for Cy.
- Ben gains nothing for himself and takes the owl for Ana; Ana gains the moth for the colony.
- Next night: no complaint to carry, since every squeak was a gift.

**The plan by night 5, and what breaks it:** "one bat far away squeaks all night to hold the owl".
The owl goes for the most recent squeak, so a hunter who squeaks to see a moth takes it off the decoy at once; nobody can hunt without squeaking.

**Between nights:** the colony grows with every moth brought back, so tomorrow's count is higher; later nights add wind that carries a squeak sideways, rain that hides it, and a second owl.

**With 1 human + bots:** a squeak key does what the mic does, so bots can play both sides.

**Marks:** lost in the dark ✅ · dread ✅ · relief from a friend's squeak ❌ (no game found) · the laugh that lights the cave ❌ (no game found).

**Unproven**
- Two of the four feelings have no shipped game behind them, which is the flag that stopped the Moonsick premise.
- Flying in the dark may be hard to steer and to sync online; the same rule works on foot (cavers whose lamps died, seeing by claps) at some cost to the fantasy (Claude).

**Near games:** Echophobia (a party game with one blind hunter, not released); HUSHED and Voidwood (co-op, the monster hears the mic).

## 5. Last Round


**After critic round 1: dropped.** The drunk is asked to want home and to want the kebab; Who's Your Daddy works because the baby wins by dying. Passing out on purpose and being carried is the best plan. It is the user's dropped family of one shared thing kept going while events are dealt. Five escorts are idle at 6. Friendly Steps, Hobble-Wobble and Groggers! have the premise.
**Fantasy:** one of us is very drunk, and the rest of us have to get them home before sunrise.

**Feelings, and the games that already cause them**
| Feeling | Games that cause it | How they do it | How it fails there | A shipped game causes it? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mischief: I'm the problem tonight | Who's Your Daddy?! | one player is a baby who heads for every danger in the house | balance and clunky controls | ✅ (as a two-sided game) |
| Exasperated care: I have to stop this idiot | Who's Your Daddy?! | the other player is the father who has to prevent it | same | ✅ (as a two-sided game) |
| The morning after: "you did what?" | none searched | | | ❌ not searched |

Sources: [SteamSpy, 2 to 5 million owners](https://www.steamspy.com/app/427730), [Kotaku on its rise](https://kotaku.com/baby-killing-game-is-so-hot-right-now-1747822027), [Pure Xbox, still a top paid game in 2022](https://www.purexbox.com/news/2022/08/whos-your-daddy-is-still-one-of-xboxs-most-popular-games).

**Job:** walk the street home.

**How the drunk is on the crew's side:** the drunk has an urge meter; an unmet urge brings them closer to passing out, and a passed-out friend has to be carried by two.
The drunk's screen shows the urges as wonderful and hides the dangers (a police car looks like a taxi).

**The choice (both sides decide):** the drunk goes for the kebab stand, or holds it in; the friends let them have this one and lose minutes to it, or drag them past while they fight the drag and slide toward passing out.

**The street, one rule:** temptations are dealt from a short list onto a generated street.

**Scene, 4 friends**
- Dee is the drunk; she sees a glowing taxi and runs for it. Ana sees a police car and grabs her arm.
- Ben says let her have the kebab instead; Cy says there is no time.
