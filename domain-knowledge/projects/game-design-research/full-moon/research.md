## Identify-with-tools (Phasmophobia)
- Each ghost type has 3 evidence types from a pool of 7; players eliminate in a journal [source: https://www.exitlag.com/blog/phasmophobia-cheat-sheet/].
- Players lean on cheat sheets (too many types to memorize); harder difficulties hide evidence (Nightmare shows 2 of 3), forcing behaviour tests [source: same + search summary].
- Developers reshuffled evidence types, making memorized combinations obsolete [source: search summary, gamerjournalist].
- Critical review: identification feels "either too easy or nearly impossible" [source: search summary of a third-party review].
- Lesson [opinion]: a fixed symptom→creature table gets memorized; the % per symptom (user's U4) is the right defence, and the difficulty must sit between "obvious" and "impossible".

## Taking players out of play
- R.E.P.O. forum (19 Apr 2025): "there are often 2-3 dead players watching 1-2 non dead players play the game"; replies: dead as ghosts who help, briefly stun a monster [source: https://steamcommunity.com/app/3241660/discussions/0/597398551725612718].
- R.E.P.O. later added Death Head possession + battery so eliminated players help survivors [source: search summary, ingamenews.com 2025-10]; DeathHeadHopper mod ~4M downloads [source: thunderstore].
- DRS gameplay-anti-patterns #2: player elimination forces downtime; punish by changing how someone plays, not whether [source: ~/Playground/deathly-reality-show/docs/gameplay-anti-patterns.md].

## Knocking out / carrying a friend (PEAK)
- "Unconscious Scouts can also be carried around, which temporarily stops the death timer unless you have over 50 Spores"; carrying adds Weight; passed-out players can't move or use items and friends can't hear them [source: https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/How_to_play].
- Lesson [opinion]: a pause on a friend's timer works when it costs the helper something (weight, a slot), and has a limit (the 50-spore cap).
