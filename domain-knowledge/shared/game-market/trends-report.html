Summary

Seven findings

  1. 1

    The audience keeps growing. The combined peak of players in friendslop games went from about 197k (Lethal Company, Dec 2023) to 281k (R.E.P.O., Mar 2025) to about 366k (MECCHA CHAMELEON, Jun 2026). How to Fish alone hit 374k in August.

  2. 2

    Revenue per hit is shrinking. Prices fell from $8–10 to $6–8, and 2026’s top hits grossed about $40–60M against R.E.P.O.’s $152M lifetime.

  3. 3

    It is hit-or-nothing. GameDiscoverCo sees “very few middling friendslop hits”. Mid-size hits (15–20k peak players) die in 1–3 months; Mage Arena is down to 72 players.

  4. 4

    Every game loses most of its players fast. Typical drop: 50–90% within 2 months, about 3% still playing after 30 days. Only games with big updates (R.E.P.O., PEAK, Gamble With Your Friends) keep a long tail.

  5. 5

    Horror is fading; familiar formats are rising. 2026’s hits were hide-and-seek, golf, fishing, gambling and a job sim, not horror. Mic gimmicks and lo-fi horror looks are declining.

  6. 6

    Buyers are young, PC-first, and buy as a group. One friend suggests it, the group buys together, plays about 3–4 hours, and moves on within weeks.

  7. 7

    The biggest new risk is AI-assisted cloning, with dozens of clones appearing within days of a viral hit. Being first, a distinctive art style and an early demo are the defences.

Buyers

Who buys, and how

Who

  • Young: 12% of YAPYAP players are 35+, against 27% across Steam fans surveyed. Commentators call friendslop “Roblox coming to Steam”.
  • Where: US leads (25–47%), then Germany, Brazil, UK, Canada; Russia and China rank high for PEAK. In one 2026 game, Brazil was over 30% of demo players.
  • Asia via VTubers: CloverPit’s Hololive coverage made Japan and Korea 9% each. MECCHA CHAMELEON debuted #1 in Japan.
  • Languages worth having: English, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, German, Japanese, Korean.
  • Platforms: PC first; Switch 2 and PS5 ports 6–12 months later (MECCHA sold 300k on Switch 2 in a week).
  • Gender split: not found.

How they buy and play

  • As a group: one friend suggests it, the rest buy with “little hesitation”. A group of four spends $20–80.
  • Price paid: How to Fish averaged about $4.95 a copy against $7.99 list (discounts, regional pricing).
  • Playtime: median 3h53m (PEAK), 3h10m (Roadside Research).
  • Retention: D1 ~20%, D7 ~4%, D30 ~3%. 61% of PEAK players also played R.E.P.O.: the same crowd moves between games.
  • Refunds: under-2-hour games get refunded (21% in one case); CloverPit’s 6.4% beat the 9.5% median.
  • Spending after launch: almost none; cosmetics and DLC data not found.

How they find games

  • Short video: Instagram Reels and TikTok over-index; YouTube Shorts underperformed for CloverPit. Mage Arena grew from TikTok.
  • Streamers: they came back to Super Battle Golf 5–6 times in three weeks. MECCHA reached 18M Twitch hours. Streams create spikes, not long play.
  • Wishlists matter less for viral hits: PEAK launched with 29k. Dear Passengers got 1.5M from a 45M-view trailer before anyone played it.
  • Demos: large play counts, weaker conversion (a group makes one decision). Roadside Research: 903k demo players → 396k sales.
  • Next Fest is getting harder: a top-10% demo gained +63 followers in Oct 2026, against +173 a year earlier; 7,240 demos, up from 2,963.

What buyers are tired of, and what they reward

  • Not tired of the genre: Zukowski says the audience is “hungry, not saturated” and buys something new every couple of months.
  • Tired of each individual hook: clones with no twist, lo-fi horror built for streamers, animal skins on a weak verb, AI-made slop.
  • Forgiving of jank: RV There Yet? sold 1M+ in week one without controller support. Not forgiving of short length.
  • Rewards a finished game with free updates over a live service.

Shape of 2027

  • 1–2 megahits, each peaking at 300–400k players and holding the crown 3–9 months.
  • An even thinner middle tier: most games either break out or sell little.
  • Prices stay at $5–8; group buying keeps them low.
  • Releases keep rising (Zukowski projects ~40k Steam releases in 2026), so discovery gets harder.
  • Clones within days, often AI-assisted, plus free Roblox copies for the youngest players.

What this means for a new friendslop game

  • A theme alone won’t stand out by 2027. Lead with a familiar format or a job and a funny shared-failure hook.
  • Pick a verb outside the crowded lanes (see the radar’s open list).
  • Plan for a 1–3 month spike, priced $5–8, with free updates ready to extend it.
  • Use a distinctive art style and post short clips early: the main defence against clones.
  • Localise for Brazil, Russia, China, Japan and Korea; VTubers drive discovery in Japan and Korea.
