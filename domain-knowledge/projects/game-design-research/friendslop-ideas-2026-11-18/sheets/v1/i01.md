# Proposal for the critic: Gold Fever

**Fantasy:** We stole a king's cursed ring, so everything we touch turns to gold and the gold climbs our own bodies; only a friend's grip can take it off me, and we want to sail away rich before we are statues.
One-player test: Alone nobody can take my gold, so I am a statue after a few crates; the line doesn't work with one player.

**Players:** 3–6, one crew. **A session:** one night is about 10 minutes; a run is night after night until a quota is missed; a sitting holds 3–6 nights.

**Feelings the game is known for** (not played yet; the feelings aimed at)
"Take it, take it, it's at my neck!"
"Not my hand, I'm carrying, put it in my leg."
"We're short. Sell Ben."
"Ben is hopping away from the scale."

**The rules, one sentence each**
Touching junk in the ship's hold turns it to gold, and each touch adds gold to the hand that touched.
Gold on a body creeps on by itself: hand, arm, chest, the other arm, the legs, then the head.
A golden part stops working: a golden hand can't grip or turn junk, a golden leg drags at half speed, two golden legs can't walk, and a golden head makes you a statue.
A statue can only hop slowly and can't touch, grip or carry.
Only a friend can take your gold: you grip one part of a friend for 2 seconds and your gold flows into that part, and the friend can pull away before the 2 seconds end.
Golden junk counts only when it is carried up to the scale on deck, and a carrier needs two working hands for a crate and two working legs to kick a barrel.
Every golden thing brought aboard makes the ship sit lower, so water rises in the hold as the haul grows.
A friend with a golden leg who stands in water above the knee is stuck until a friend with two working hands pulls them out, which takes 4 seconds.
A statue friend weighs as gold: the crew can carry them to the scale and sell them for about a quarter of a night's quota, and a sold friend sits out the next night unless the crew buys them back at double.
A statue carried to the cabin instead is safe, and dawn clears all gold from every body.
The quota rises every night, and a missed quota ends the run.
Each night the hold holds a different mix of crates, barrels and two-carrier chests.

**How much there is:** one ship (a hold, a deck with the scale, a cabin), no creatures, 3 kinds of junk, no items; cash buys back sold friends and nothing else.

**Voice:** none in the game; the friends are on a voice app where everyone hears everyone.

**The choices**
Touch one more crate and let the gold reach my shoulder, or stop and haul what is already gold?
Ana grips me to hand over her gold: do I give her my hand, which ends my carrying, or my leg, which drags and will stick in the water later?
The water is at our knees and Cy is stuck with a golden leg: pull him out for 4 seconds with both hands, or get the last crate up to the scale?
Ben is a statue at 3 in the morning and we are short by about his weight: carry him to the scale, or to the cabin?
My gold is at my chest: pass it now to the friend with the least gold, or keep touching until it reaches my neck?

**What the other end decides in the same second**
The friend being gripped chooses which part to turn toward the grip, or pulls away.
The stuck friend drops what they hold to be pulled faster, or keeps it.
The statue hops toward the scale or toward the cabin while two friends decide where to carry them.
The friend with the least gold steps in to take it, or stays out of reach to keep both hands.

**Scene, 4 friends**
- Ana has touched five crates and her right arm is gold to the shoulder; she can't lift.
- She grips Ben, who turns his left leg to her; her arm clears, and Ben now drags a golden leg.
- Four golden crates later the hold is knee-deep; Ben's leg sticks, and he is holding a crate.
- Cy has two good hands but is halfway up the stairs with a barrel; Dee's hands are both gold.
- Cy kicks the barrel up, comes back, and pulls Ben for 4 seconds while the water reaches Ben's waist.
- Dee's gold reaches her head on the stairs; she is a statue, the scale reads just under the quota, and it is nearly dawn.
- Ana and Cy lift Dee; Dee starts hopping toward the cabin.

**What experienced crews do:** (not played; Claude's guess) touch in turns, park gold in legs early while the hold is dry, and move it to arms once the water comes.
**What changes it from round to round:** the mix of junk decides which parts are worth keeping, and the quota decides how much gold the crew must wear.
**What carries from one round to the next:** cash, the rising quota, and any friend who was sold.
**Can a new crew lose? Does it get harder?** Yes: everyone a statue, or a missed quota. The quota rises, and a bigger haul floods the hold sooner.
