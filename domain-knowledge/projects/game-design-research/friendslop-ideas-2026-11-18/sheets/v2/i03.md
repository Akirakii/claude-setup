# Proposal for the critic: Piggyback

**Fantasy:** We gather cure-herbs in a night marsh for a plague village; only the sick can see the herbs glow and only the healthy can see the sinking mud, so the sick ride on a friend's back and point, and we want one more bloom before the one carrying is as sick as the one riding.
One-player test: Alone, healthy I see no herbs, and sick enough to see the good ones I can't walk or see the mud; the line doesn't work with one player.

**Players:** 3–6, one crew. **A session:** one night is about 10 minutes; a run is night after night until a quota is missed; a sitting holds 3–6 nights.

**Feelings the game is known for** (not played yet; the feelings aimed at)
"Left. Left! No, MY left."
"Stop, STOP, there's mud right in front of you two."
"Is that one real?" "I don't know!"
"You're coughing on my neck, I can't see the ground any more."

**The rules, one sentence each**
The fever has four stages; a friend catches stage 1 on purpose by drinking from the marsh, and every friend starts each night at the stage they ended the last one.
At stage 0 you see no herbs but you see the sinking mud; from stage 1 the mud looks like firm ground to you.
At stage 1 the red moss glows for you, at stage 2 the blue reeds, worth three times as much, and at stage 3 the silver blooms, worth nine.
At stage 2 you walk at half speed, at stage 3 you can't walk at all, and at stage 4 you pass out and are a body to be dragged.
A friend who can walk can carry one friend on their back; the rider points with an arm, and the carrier walks and picks with both hands.
Carrying fills the carrier's fever: 30 seconds of carrying in total, however it is split up, raises the carrier one stage.
Your own fever also rises by itself, one stage every 3 minutes, from the moment you catch it.
A rider at stage 3 also sees blooms that aren't there, and can't tell them from the real ones until the carrier's hands close on nothing.
A bundle of herbs lowers your fever one stage only if it is the colour of your own stage: red for stage 1, blue for 2, silver for 3.
Anyone who walks into mud sinks and is stuck until a friend pulls them out, which takes 4 seconds and both hands; a carrier and rider sink together.
Herbs bloom in new places every night, and the mud moves with them.
The basket is weighed at dawn against a quota that rises every night, and a missed quota ends the run.
A friend who is passed out at dawn starts the next night at stage 2.

**How much there is:** one marsh, generated herb and mud placement, no creatures, 3 kinds of herb, no items, no shop.

**Voice:** none in the game; the friends are on a voice app where everyone hears everyone.

**The choices**
Riding at stage 2: eat a blue bundle and walk again, or let it climb to stage 3 for the silver blooms and need a back to ride?
Carrying: put the rider down with 5 of my 30 seconds left, or spend them on the bloom they are pointing at, which may not be there?
Clean, with the basket: walk ahead of the pair calling the mud, or leave them and pull the two who are already stuck?
My rider is at stage 3 and we hold one silver bundle, worth nine: feed it to them so they walk, or bank it and keep carrying?
Who drinks from the marsh tonight, and who stays clean to see the ground?

**What the other end decides in the same second**
The rider points at the near cheap herb or the far rich one, and says whether they trust this bloom.
The carrier walks where the arm points, or stops because the clean friend shouted.
The clean friend chooses which pair to walk ahead of, since they can only see the mud near themselves.
The stuck rider gets off into the mud so the carrier comes free faster, or stays on.

**Scene, 4 friends**
- Ana drank first, let her fever reach stage 3, and rides on Ben; Cy is clean and walks ahead with the basket; Dee drank late and is at stage 1.
- Ana points across the marsh at a silver bloom; Cy says "mud on your right, come round by me".
- Ben reaches it at 22 of his 30 seconds, picks it, and it is real; Ana points at a second one further on.
- Cy has gone to pull Dee, who walked into mud she couldn't see while chasing red moss.
- Ben goes for the second bloom with nobody calling the ground; his hands close on nothing, his 30 seconds run out, and he is at stage 1.
- Now Ben sees red moss everywhere and the mud nowhere; he takes two steps toward the moss and sinks, with Ana on his back.
- Cy has one pair of hands, two stuck pairs, and three minutes until dawn.

**What experienced crews do:** (not played; Claude's guess) one rider at stage 3, one clean friend who never drinks and walks point, and the rest take turns as the back and eat red on the way home.
**What changes it from round to round:** where the rich herbs and the mud are, and which stage each friend carried over.
**What carries from one round to the next:** each friend's fever stage, and cash toward the rising quota.
**Can a new crew lose? Does it get harder?** Yes: a crew with nobody clean walks into the mud, and a crew that all reaches stage 3 can't move. The quota rises until red moss alone can't pay it.
