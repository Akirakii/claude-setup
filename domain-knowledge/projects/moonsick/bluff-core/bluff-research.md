## Where it would shine in Moonsick (Claude, [opinion])
Where only one person can know a fact and the others must pay to check it.
The best fit is the sick friend's own feelings (only they feel it), the user's original pillar "the patient can lie
about inner signs" (U14).
Checking would cost a trip or an item, and believing a liar costs the friend's turn.
It's missing two parts today: a reason to lie (questions.md 17), and the private symptoms the user parked.
A wrong item with no penalty (step 1) removes the penalty part, so a lie costs nothing to call.

## Does the variety of items make more bluff? (2026-11-14, Claude)
User: "doest hte variaety of current items help to create more bluff opportunities ?"
Only partly. A bluff needs something only you know, so an item adds bluff only when its symptom can be hidden:
- bucket (nausea), coffee (sleepiness), dog cone (awoo): ✅ holding one could mean you're secretly sick or hoarding.
- sunglasses (eyes), razor (fur), dog bone (drool), perfume (stink), walker (four legs): ❌ their symptoms show on the
  body, so these add trading, not bluff.
Pick (Claude): make about half the symptoms hideable, so the number of items stops mattering and what you hold always
leaves people guessing.

## Making the failing lies work (2026-11-14, Claude; user: "how to make core bluff lie on: A wrong item on purpose and Faking a symptom, or lying about which one")
Both fail line 2 (a reason to lie): treating only removes a symptom, so being given an item is worth nothing to a
friend without that symptom, and a wrong item gains the giver nothing.

**Wrong item on purpose → a test (Claude, Sheriff-style inspection).** Give an item to a friend who says "I'm
fine": if they're hiding that symptom, it cures them with a visible puff; if not, the slapstick burst shows and they

## "Whoever is wrong pays": other games vs Moonsick (2026-11-14, Claude)
User: "so how in other games this happens and why that doesnt happen in moonsick?"
Other games: a call is one moment, the truth is shown at once, and a fixed cost lands on the liar or the wrong caller.
Coup: a challenged card is flipped, and the liar or the challenger loses a card [source: Mechanics of Magic].
Liar's Dice: all dice are shown, and the wrong side loses a die [source: Mechanics of Magic].
Sheriff of Nottingham: the bag is opened; smuggling pays the sheriff, an honest bag gets paid by the sheriff [source: Medium].
Why Moonsick lacks it [read: design docs]:
- No moment that shows the truth: holding shows veins only roughly, and the user's pillar says liars are never exposed (U22).
- The truth only comes out when someone turns, and then the cost lands on whoever stands near, not on whoever believed.
- Checking costs only time, and a wrong doubt costs the doubter nothing.
