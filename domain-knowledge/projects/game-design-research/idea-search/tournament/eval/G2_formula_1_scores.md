## Patterns
- **Commonest F3 loss: a call turns one end into an obeyer.** In 10 ideas (G203, G205, G206, G207, G211, G227, G229, G233, G251, G253), a voice call ("now", "slow", "go") lets one seat just obey or hold, and the "both decide at once" coupling the text claims goes away.
- **Same job plus interference is not coupling.** G219, G231 and G239 give everyone the same job and connect players only by getting in each other's way. In each, a simple convention (empty end, adjacent stands, track the bull) removes the coupling, so F3 is ✗. G212 and G217 come close, but avoiding each other is still their dominant play.
- **F7 is still the commonest lost point.** 29 of 33 ideas take ~ or ✗ on F7, because their Tails list "generated layout + random parameters" on a procedure the crew solves in the first runs. Only player-made state that piles up (set foam G203, the rope web G215, crumbled holds G225, frozen posts G249) earned ✓. G219 and G241 have no Tail line at all.
- **Instant-sync rules are frequent and carry lag risk.** 12 ideas hinge on two players' inputs meeting in "the same instant" (jump/duck, land/launch, slide/let go, hop/hop, plug/arrive, open/pass). They are the ones that sound most coupled, and they are also the most fragile online.
- **The batch repeats itself.** There are 7 same-core pairs: stone on rollers (G211/G227), jump/duck crossings (G213/G233), zero-g recoil (G237/G247), carts rolling into sidings (G208/G221), a landing that launches a friend (G209/G245), a rate-setter with station keepers (G207/G253), and an interference field pushed onto a teammate's delicate job (G212/G217). Only 4 ideas have no ✗ and lose a point on only one check (G210, G221, G225, G235), and none is strict.
