## The problem (user, 2026-11-14)
"The game can be countered by having all people around the sick one with all items in hand and just hand over based
on what they see." And: "the game obstacle is to find the objects.. but that is not hard and shouldnt be.. so there
is no fun like other games."

In general terms: a need shows on one player and the others supply the matching fix.
When every fix is small, visible and carryable, the crew carries all of them and waits, and no need is ever hard.
Something has to stop the fix being ready before the need shows, without making the search the challenge.

Premise check [read, `docs/symptoms-thesis.md`]: the symptoms sims already found that one player alone can handle
every case for the first two thirds of the night, so the counter holds even without camping.
