# Proposal for the critic: Piggyback

**Fantasy:** We gather cure-herbs in a night marsh for a plague village, and only the sick can see the herbs glow, so the sick ride on a healthy friend's back and point, and we want one more basket before the carrier is as sick as the rider.
One-player test: Alone, healthy I see no herbs, and sick enough to see the good ones I can't walk; the line doesn't work with one player.

**Players:** 3–6, one crew. **A session:** one night is about 10 minutes; a run is night after night until a quota is missed; a sitting holds 3–6 nights.

**Feelings the game is known for** (not played yet; the feelings aimed at)
"Left. Left! No, MY left."
"You're coughing on my neck, I can see the blue ones now."
"One more stage and I'll see the silver ones. Carry me."
"We're both down. Somebody come and drag us."

**The rules, one sentence each**
The fever has four stages, and every friend starts each night at the stage they ended the last one.
At stage 0 you see no herbs; at stage 1 the common red moss glows for you; at stage 2 the blue reeds, worth three times as much; at stage 3 the silver blooms, worth nine.
At stage 2 you walk at half speed, at stage 3 you can't walk at all, and at stage 4 you pass out and are a body to be dragged.
A friend who can walk can carry one friend on their back; the rider sees and points with an arm, and the carrier walks and picks with both hands.
A carrier breathes the rider's fever: every 30 seconds of carrying raises the carrier one stage.
Your fever also rises by itself, one stage every 3 minutes, from the moment you first catch it.
Eating herbs from the basket lowers your fever one stage per bundle, and those bundles are gone from the haul.
The marsh has sinking mud: a carrier with a rider sinks in it and is stuck until another friend pulls them out, which takes 4 seconds and both hands.
Herbs bloom in new places every night, and the mud moves with them.
The basket is weighed at dawn against a quota that rises every night, and a missed quota ends the run.
A friend who is passed out at dawn starts the next night at stage 2.

**How much there is:** one marsh, generated herb and mud placement, no creatures, 3 kinds of herb, no items, no shop.

**Voice:** none in the game; the friends are on a voice app where everyone hears everyone.

**The choices**
Riding at stage 2: eat a bundle and walk again, or let it climb to stage 3 for the silver blooms and need a back to ride?
Carrying: put the rider down now at 25 seconds, or take the extra stage to reach the bloom they are pointing at?
Two friends are down in the mud and I have the basket: go back and drag them out, or pick the moss I can see alone?
We are over the quota by two bundles and Ben is at stage 3: spend three bundles to stand him up, or carry him home and start tomorrow with him sick?
Who catches the fever first tonight, and who stays clean to carry?

**What the other end decides in the same second**
The rider points at the near cheap herb or the far rich one, and says when their fever is about to tip over.
The carrier walks where the arm points, or refuses and turns for home.
The stuck pair chooses whether the rider gets off into the mud to free the carrier.
The clean friend chooses which sick friend to pick up first.

**Scene, 4 friends**
- Ana let her fever reach stage 3 and rides on Ben; she points across the mud at a silver bloom nobody else can see.
- Ben walks, counting his own 30 seconds; he reaches the bloom at 28 and picks it with both hands.
- At 30 Ben is at stage 1 and sees red moss everywhere; he wants to stop for it, and Ana points home.
- Ben cuts across the mud and sinks, with Ana on his back.
- Cy, clean, has the basket and is forty steps away; Dee is at stage 2 and walks at half speed toward them.
- Cy pulls for 4 seconds; Ben comes free at stage 2 and can't carry Ana any further at more than half speed.
- Dawn is two minutes off, Ana is at stage 3 on the ground, and the basket is one bundle over the quota.

**What experienced crews do:** (not played; Claude's guess) one friend rides at stage 3 all night, the others take 25-second turns as the back, and the last bundles over the quota are eaten before dawn.
**What changes it from round to round:** where the rich herbs and the mud are, and which stage each friend carried over.
**What carries from one round to the next:** each friend's fever stage, cash toward the rising quota.
**Can a new crew lose? Does it get harder?** Yes: a crew that all reaches stage 3 can't move. The quota rises until red moss alone can't pay it.
