# The ladder: ten shipped co-op designs by how many friend groups played them

The sheets are in `sheets/` under made-up names (`g11`–`g14` are the four games kept aside for the check; the judge doesn't read them). The tier is from each game's Steam review count (measured 2026-11-18).
Within a tier the order is by review count, most first.

| Tier | Reviews | Sheets |
|---|---|---|
| A: a huge hit | over 300,000 | g01, g02, g03 |
| B: a hit | 50,000 to 80,000 | g04, g05 |
| C: a small audience | 4,000 to 11,000 | g06, g07, g08 |
| D: almost nobody | under 500 | g09, g10 |

## When each came out, and whether its core act was new then
"New" means no earlier well-known co-op game had this as its core act (Claude's judgement, made after seeing the review counts, so it leans toward the result).

| Sheet | Came out | Core act | New when it came out? |
|---|---|---|---|
| g01 | Oct 2023 | haul junk out past creatures for a rising quota, voice by distance | ✅ |
| g04 | Apr 2024 | film a friend in danger, then watch the clip together | ✅ |
| g06 | May 2024 | hide from one creature that hears the microphone | ❌ follows older hide-and-escape horror |
| g05 | Jun 2024 | climb while chained to your friends | ✅ |
| g08 | Dec 2024 | g01's haul, under water, with a submarine | ❌ follows g01 |
| g09 | Jan 2025 | g01's haul, under water | ❌ follows g01 |
| g07 | Jan 2025 | photograph creatures and friends for a score | ❌ follows g04 |
| g02 | Feb 2025 | carry fragile loot together with physics | ✅ |
| g03 | Jun 2025 | climb, pulling and boosting each other | ✅ |
| g10 | Jul 2025 | g01's haul, as food orders read over a radio | ❌ follows g01 |

In this list every sheet with a new core act is in tier A or B, and every sheet that follows an earlier one is in tier C or D.
The same holds for the four games kept aside (a 2020 first of its kind and a 2025 road trip in a shared vehicle: A and B; two 2024 followers: C and D).

What a sheet can't show: its price, who streamed it, and how well it is built. A place on the ladder is a guess from the rules, the date and the novelty only.
