## Passed (5)
1. **Sin Eaters:** taking a friend's sin frees them, but the sin makes *you* act it out (hit, grab, slow), so the
   crew's state keeps scrambling.
2. **Dancing Plague:** you save a dancer by dancing them down, and you catch the dance; it spreads by contact.
3. **Burning Set (weak):** tackling a burning friend can set you alight. Same core as Werewolf Pack.
4. **Berserker Clan (weak):** holding a raging friend passes the rage to you. Same core as Werewolf Pack; raiders are content.
5. **Sleepwalkers (weak):** waking a friend makes you drowsy; weak because the sleeper doesn't decide anything.

## Failed (11)
- **Beekeepers' Queen** (no fixed plan): "the queen goes to whoever is nearest the attack and least stung".
- **Symbiote** (same core as Beekeepers' Queen).

- **Shadow Troupe** (probe): "a ring on each other's shadows". **Siren Song** (probe): "a ring of hands over ears".

- **Gorgon Sisters** (probe): "back to back". **Debt Devils** (probe): "balance the debts".

## Findings
- **The early probe did its job:** 8 of 16 dropped before the checks, all to a one-line plan: lookup ("least
  infected helps"), ring formation, or a learnable table.
- **Rings beat "the help moves the harm"** when everyone can help at once (shadows, ears).
- **The survivors keep landing on one core:** restrain or treat a struggling friend, and the harm spreads by
  contact (Werewolf, Burning Set, Berserker, Dancing Plague). That is an attractor, so these are mostly one idea.
- **Sin Eaters is the new shape:** the harm changes how the carrier *behaves* toward the others, not just a meter.
  That kept its plan from settling.
