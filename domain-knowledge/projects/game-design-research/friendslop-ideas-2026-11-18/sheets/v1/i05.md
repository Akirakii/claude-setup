# Proposal for the critic: Moonsick, the wolf we need

**Fantasy:** It is full moon in a mansion with friends, and the silver we came for is behind doors only a werewolf can break, so one of us has to turn; I fear being the one who stays turned too long and stops being me among my friends.
One-player test: Alone, nobody can bring me back once I turn, so the first door is my last; the line doesn't work with one player.

**Players:** 3–6, one crew. **A session:** one night is about 10 minutes; a run is night after night until a quota is missed; a sitting holds 3–6 nights.

**Feelings the game is known for** (not played yet; the feelings aimed at)
"One more door. I've still got it. I've still got it."
"He's drifting toward you, get behind the table."
"Bring me back NOW, I can't turn away from you."
"You said one more door."

**The rules, one sentence each**
The mansion's silver is in rooms behind oak doors, and only a wolf can break an oak door; friends carry the silver out to the cart.
Any friend can turn by stepping into the moonbeam in the hall, and at most the crew needs one wolf at a time.
A fresh wolf is fully mine: I run, break doors and stop as I like.
Every door I break and every 20 seconds as a wolf adds one notch of hunger, and I see my own notches.
At each notch my wolf pulls harder toward the nearest friend: at 2 notches it drifts, at 4 I must keep steering away to hold a straight line, at 6 I can no longer turn away at all.
A wolf that touches a friend bites: the friend drops their silver, is knocked down for 5 seconds, and my hunger falls by 2 notches.
A friend bitten three times in one night is out until dawn.
Two friends with empty hands who hold the wolf for 3 seconds turn it back, and the turned-back friend can't turn again for 2 minutes.
A wolf at 6 notches can only be held by three friends.
Which rooms hold the silver changes every night, and deeper rooms hold more behind more doors.
The cart's quota rises every night, and a missed quota ends the run.
A friend who was out at dawn starts the next night already bitten once.

**How much there is:** one mansion with generated silver rooms, no creatures other than the friends, one kind of loot in three sizes, no items, no shop.

**Voice:** none in the game; the friends are on a voice app where everyone hears everyone.

**The choices**
As the wolf at 3 notches with one door left before the big room: break it, or call my friends to bring me back now?
As a carrier with the wolf drifting at me: put the silver down and get away, or keep carrying and take the bite?
As the crew: put two carriers' silver down to hold the wolf now, at 3 notches, or get the load to the cart first and need three of us later?
As the wolf at 5 notches: run for the far wing, away from everyone, where nobody can reach me in time, or stay near and risk the bite?
Who turns tonight: the friend bitten twice already, who is safe as a wolf, or the best carrier?

**What the other end decides in the same second**
The friends near the wolf choose to hold it now or keep carrying.
The carrier being drifted at drops the silver or shields it by taking the bite on purpose, which also buys the wolf 2 notches.
The wolf steers its pull so the bite lands on the friend with empty hands, not the one with the big silver.
The friend bitten twice decides whether to be the third holder.

**Scene, 4 friends**
- Ana turns in the hall beam and breaks two doors; Ben, Cy and Dee carry silver from the first room.
- Ana is at 3 notches and drifting; the big room is one door further; she breaks it and is at 4.
- Ben and Cy run in for the big silver; Ana holds a line away from them by steering hard and says "come and get me after this load".
- Twenty seconds later she is at 5 and her wolf curves toward Dee, who is carrying alone in the corridor.
- Dee sets the silver down and backs behind a table; Ana clips her anyway: Dee is down for 5 seconds, and Ana drops to 3 notches.
- Ben and Cy put the big silver down in the doorway and hold Ana for 3 seconds; she is herself again and can't turn for 2 minutes.
- There are two doors left, nobody is a wolf, and Cy, never bitten tonight, walks to the hall beam.

**What experienced crews do:** (not played; Claude's guess) the wolf breaks two doors and comes back to be held, a different friend turns next, and bites are taken on purpose by whoever has empty hands.
**What changes it from round to round:** where the silver rooms are, how many doors each needs, and who starts already bitten.
**What carries from one round to the next:** cash toward the rising quota, and one bite on anyone who was out at dawn.
**Can a new crew lose? Does it get harder?** Yes: a wolf at 6 notches with fewer than three friends standing ends the night. The quota rises until two doors per turn can't pay it.
