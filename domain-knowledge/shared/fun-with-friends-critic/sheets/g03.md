# Proposal for the critic: Summit Scouts

**Fantasy:** A scout troop's plane has crashed on an island, and the scouts must climb its mountain to the top to be rescued.
One-player test: The line still works alone, and the game can be started with one player, but pulling up, boosting, carrying and reviving need a second scout.

**Players:** 1–4, one crew. **A session:** one run is one climb through six stages; the length of a run is (not found), and the game autosaves at each campfire so a run can be continued later.

**Feelings the game is known for** (2–4, each as a line a player says to a friend)
- (not found in the sources as player quotes; the lines below restate what the rules produce.)
- "I ran out of stamina one arm's length below the ledge and you pulled me up."
- "We ate the strange berry because we were starving, and it poisoned us."
- "The fog was right behind us and one of us was still at the bottom of the wall."

**The rules, one sentence each** (every rule a player must know; as many as the game really has, 8–14)
- Any surface can be climbed, and climbing, sprinting and lunging drain one stamina bar that refills when the scout rests on a ledge.
- If stamina runs out during a climb, the scout slides off and falls, and falls cause injury.
- Hunger, injury, carried weight, poison, cold, heat, drowsiness and other afflictions each take up part of the same bar, so less stamina is left to use.
- Hunger grows over time and is removed by eating; injury stays until treated; poison fades by itself; weight goes away only by dropping or using items.
- When afflictions fill the whole bar the scout passes out, cannot move, use items or be heard, and dies after a limited time unless helped.
- A teammate can carry a passed-out scout, which stops the death timer, or treat them with a healing item.
- A dead scout becomes a ghost and comes back only at the revival statue at the end of a stage or through a rare revival item.
- Each scout has three item slots plus one backpack slot that holds four more items, and nothing can be held in the hands while climbing.
- Food and items are found in the wild and in suitcases from the plane; some food gives extra stamina that is spent after the main bar, and cooking food makes it restore more.
- A scout standing above a climbing teammate can reach down and pull them up, and a crouching scout can boost a teammate who stands on them.
- Ropes and wall anchors can be placed to make a route or a rest point for the others.
- Each stage ends at a campfire that can be lit only when every living player stands near it; lighting it heals some injury, gives extra stamina, saves the game and opens the way to the next stage.
- A fog rises behind the crew after a set time or height and causes cold to anyone inside it; it pauses while the whole crew is at a lit campfire.
- A scout who climbs far above the rest of the group is attacked by a tall pursuing figure.

**How much there is:** Six stages per run chosen from about ten biome kinds (beach, then jungle or mushroom forest, then snow or desert, then volcano or haunted ruins, then the summit); the mountain is generated and replaced for all players once every 24 hours; an easy setting, a standard setting and eight harder tiers; about fourteen afflictions; creature kinds seen in sources are zombies, a spider, a grabbing plant and the pursuing figure; total item count (not found).

**Voice:** Voice by distance, with push-to-talk or push-to-mute; a passed-out scout cannot be heard; a button places a ping that all players see; whether ghosts can be heard is (not found).

**The choices** (3–5 lines a player really faces, "do X, or do Y?")
- Eat the food now to remove hunger, or save it for the extra stamina on a hard wall?
- Carry more items and lose stamina to weight, or travel light and have nothing when hurt?
- Go back down for a fallen scout while the fog rises, or keep climbing and revive them at the statue?
- Climb ahead to test a route and place a rope, or stay with the group?
- Use the healing item on yourself, or on the teammate who is worse off?

**What the other end decides in the same second** (per choice, or "nothing")
- Food: the others decide whether to hand over their own food or keep it.
- Weight: the others decide who takes the backpack and which items get left behind.
- Fallen scout: the fallen scout decides nothing once passed out; the rest decide who goes down and who waits.
- Scouting ahead: the ones below decide whether to follow the same line or wait for the rope.
- Healing: the hurt teammate decides whether to ask for it or push on.

**Scene, 4 friends** (5–7 bullets, one real moment the game is known for, second by second)
- Three scouts stand on a ledge under a tall wall and the fourth, with the lightest load, starts climbing to test it.
- Halfway up, the climber's bar is nearly empty and there is no ledge, so they call down that they will not make it.
- A second scout eats a snack for extra stamina, climbs a different line, gets above the first and reaches down.
- The pull lands, both reach the top, and one drops a rope for the two below.
- The third climbs the rope; the fourth, carrying the backpack and hungry, slips, falls and passes out at the bottom.
- The fog is close, so the crew argues by voice whether one goes down to carry the fourth or all three run for the campfire and the statue.

**What experienced crews do:** They stay together, send one scout ahead to test a route and rescue them if they fall, plan routes around small ledges where stamina refills, eat food only for its extra stamina before a hard climb, and do not risk the whole group for one fallen scout because the statue can bring that scout back.
**What changes it from round to round:** The mountain layout changes every 24 hours, some stages are picked from two or more biomes, items in suitcases vary, and the crew can pick a harder tier.
**What carries from one round to the next:** Badges that unlock cosmetics, a sash for each tier finished, and the unlock of the next tier; no items or strength carry over.
**Can a new crew lose? Does it get harder?** Yes, the run ends if every scout dies; later stages add cold, heat and more creatures, and each of the eight tiers adds one lasting rule on top of the previous ones (doubled fall damage, faster hunger, heavier items, no signal flare placed at the summit, cold at night, costlier climbing, a starting curse, a required extra ending); the easy setting removes the fog, the zombies and the pursuing figure.
