# Proposal for the critic: Moonsick

**Fantasy:** It is full moon in a mansion with friends, and each of us fears turning into a werewolf that will hurt the others; the friend closest to turning is the only one who can find what saves us, so we keep each other human one more minute.
One-player test: Alone nobody shades me, nobody picks what I find, and nobody holds me and brings me back; the line doesn't work with one player.

**Players:** 3–6, one crew. **A session:** one night is about 10 minutes; a run is night after night until a mark is missed; a sitting holds 3–6 nights.

**Feelings the game is known for** (not played yet; the feelings aimed at)
"It's up to my shoulders. There's one more past the fountain, come under the cloak."
"Give me the cloak, you're too far gone."
"MOVE, MOVE, I can't stop!"
"Hold him, I'm growing too, pour it, POUR IT."

**The rules, one sentence each**
The silver cure in the cauldron is made from silver blooms, which open only where moonlight falls, in the courtyard and in rooms a moonbeam crosses.
Moonlight on you grows your fur, shade shrinks it slowly, and you see it on your own arms.
Only a friend with at least half fur can see the silver blooms, and furry hands crush them, so only a friend with less than half fur can pick one.
Each friend has a cloak: held over another friend it keeps the moon off them, but the one holding it stands in the light with both hands up.
Clouds cover and uncover the moon without warning, so the courtyard can go dark or light in one second.
At full fur you turn: you are the wolf and you play it, but it never stops running; you can only steer.
The wolf can't bear the dark, so it runs only where moonlight falls: it turns back by itself at the edge of the shade.
The wolf is faster than a friend, whatever it hits head-on it bites, and it tramples every bloom it runs over.
A bitten friend drops what they carry and gains half a body of fur.
A wolf in the light keeps growing faster the longer it runs.
Two friends who grab the wolf from the side or behind hold it, standing in the moonlight themselves, and it stays held for 3 seconds after one of them lets go a hand.
A flask of cure poured on a held wolf turns it back with no fur, and a flask poured on a furry friend takes half their fur; a flask is filled at the cauldron beforehand, lowers the cauldron, and takes one hand to carry.
The cauldron must reach the night's mark by dawn; the mark rises every night; a missed mark ends the run, and so does every friend being a wolf at once.
A friend who is still a wolf at dawn starts the next night with half fur.

**How much there is:** one mansion with a courtyard, no creatures other than the friends, one kind of bloom, two items (the cloak, the flask), no shop.

**Voice:** none in the game; the friends are on a voice app where everyone hears everyone.

**The choices**
I am the furry one and I see a bloom far out in the light: lead my clean friend to it under my cloak while my own fur climbs, or point and send them alone, unshaded?
I am the clean one and my guide is at nine tenths: take the cloak and send them to the shade, which leaves me blind to the blooms, or go for the one more they can see?
A cloud is closing: run for two more blooms while the courtyard is dark and safe, or get back before it opens with us in the middle?
Ana has turned in the courtyard: go into the light to hold her, growing my own fur, or wait in the shade while she tramples the blooms?
I hold the last flask: pour it on Ben now, at three quarters fur, or keep it for whoever turns?

**What the other end decides in the same second**
The clean friend follows the guide deeper, or stops and pulls them back.
The furry friend gives up the cloak, or keeps holding it to reach the bloom.
The wolf steers side-on past the two who came out to hold it, or misjudges and meets one head-on.
The second holder lets go a hand to pour within the 3 seconds, or keeps both hands on and waits for a third friend.

**Scene, 4 friends**
- Ana is at two thirds fur and sees three blooms nobody else can; she holds her cloak over Ben and walks him to the first.
- Ben picks it clean; Ana's fur is at three quarters; she says "one more past the fountain".
- Cy, in the doorway with a flask, says the cloud is thinning; Ben reaches for the cloak and Ana walks on.
- The cloud goes; Ana's fur fills two steps from the bloom and she turns; Ben is standing next to her with the cloak half over his head.
- Ana can't stop and can't leave the light: she steers hard round the fountain, flattens the bloom she was after, and clips Ben, who drops his flowers at half fur.
- Cy and Dee come out into the light to hold her; their own fur starts to climb; Ana shouts "left side, left side" and they catch her on the second pass.
- Dee lets go a hand and pours inside the 3 seconds; Ana is herself with no fur, and now Ben, at half fur, is the only one who can see the blooms.

**What experienced crews do:** (not played; Claude's guess) keep one guide between half and three quarters fur, swap the guide when a cloud thins, and never go out without a flask in the doorway.
**What changes it from round to round:** when the clouds open, where the blooms are, and who starts with half fur.
**What carries from one round to the next:** the rising mark, and half fur on anyone left a wolf at dawn.
**Can a new crew lose? Does it get harder?** Yes: every friend a wolf, or a missed mark. The mark rises until a guide who plays safe can't find enough blooms.
