**Choice shapes** (tag in the Shape column; added cycle 2, Claude):
- **P, push or pass:** keep the harm to make progress, or pass it to a friend / take it from one.
- **T, two ways to help:** two ways to save a friend, each costing something different.
- **U, use a friend:** use a friend's body or skill and leave them worse off.
- **S, swap harms:** cure a friend's harm by giving them a different one.
- **W, where to put it:** choose which part of a friend takes the harm, and so which ability they lose.

**Look** (added cycle 2, Claude): what the screen shows. It must show the state the choice depends on.

## Rules (cycle 2, the rule step: one sentence plus at most 2 extra rules, then the critic)
- **Sin Eaters.** Rule: each worm adds one dealt sin to your stain (Wrath: you swing at whoever is nearest; Greed: you
  can't let go of what you hold; Sloth: you move at half speed); a friend pressing bread to you draws half of each sin
  into themselves. Extra: the coffin drags in anyone whose stain is full; two friends can pull them back out.
  - Critic: by round 5 the crew writes a 3-line card: "call the sin; bread Greed and Sloth at once; for Wrath, back
    off and bread from behind when it's nearly full." What keeps it alive: half-draws mix sins, so the drawer gets a
    bit of Wrath with their Greed, and the right drawer depends on what each already carries. Verdict: low feature
    coupling ✓; no fixed plan ~.
- **Coven.** Rule: each lit candle deals one curse that names one witch (rats: a swarm walks at her and slows whoever
  it touches; fire: a ring closes on her); a cut from another witch's knife on the named witch's palm ends the curse
  at once but halves her speed until the next candle. Extra: a witch caught by the fire ring is out.
  - Critic: a 2-line card: "cut on fire; on rats, run and let the rats slow you." What keeps it alive: the next
    candle may name the slowed witch again. Verdict: low feature coupling ✓; no fixed plan ~.
- **Grave Robbers.** Rule: opening a coffin wakes its corpse, which grabs the nearest robber's leg and drags them
  toward the grave; pulling for 3 seconds frees them and the corpse grabs the puller instead; slamming the lid frees
  them at once and their rings drop back into the coffin. Extra: the watchman's lantern circles the churchyard and
  anyone in its light must freeze.
  - Critic: a 1-line card: "slam whoever holds the fewest rings; pull the rich ones." What keeps it alive: a pull
    chain (the puller now needs a puller), the lantern freezing whoever is pulling, and corpses revealed as weak or
    strong only when they grab. Verdict: low feature coupling ✓; no fixed plan ~.
