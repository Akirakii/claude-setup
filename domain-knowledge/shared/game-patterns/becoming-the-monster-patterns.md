# Becoming the monster: "it is me, but I am not in control"

Researched 2026-11-17 (web, two rounds of searches).
The problem in general terms: a player's own character becomes a danger to their teammates, the player doesn't freely choose the harm, and it still feels like them.
Tags: [source] = said by the linked source; [read] = Claude's reading across sources; [opinion] = Claude's view; (memory) = not found in this search.
None of the sources measures the feeling; they describe the rules, and a few quote players.

## Patterns

### 1. The body is taken and the player watches
- Lethal Company's Masked: the mask takes over the body, "now you're dead and in spectator mode", and the body goes on to convert others [source: [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/tips/how-to-get-and-use-dramatic-masks-in-lethal-company), [ESTNN](https://estnn.com/lethal-company-masked-what-it-is-this-terrifying-monster)].
- The Blackout Club: the Shape takes a caught player's mind, and the character becomes an NPC that patrols and alerts enemies until a teammate revives it [source: [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/the-blackout-club-stars-a-terrifying-monster-that-wouldnt-work-without-co-op), [Bit-Tech](https://bit-tech.net/reviews/the-blackout-club-preview/1)].
- Barotrauma's husk infection: four stages with warning messages (a sore throat, then movement in the throat); at the last stage the player loses the character, unless a server flag lets them keep it [source: [Barotrauma wiki](https://barotraumagame.com/wiki/Husk_Infection)].
- Evil Dead: The Game: a survivor's fear meter rises when alone or in the dark; when it is full, the demon player takes the body and attacks the team with it [source: [Twinfinite](https://twinfinite.net/guides/evil-dead-the-game-fear-meter-explained-how-decrease-what-does/), [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/evil-dead-the-game-tips-for-survivors-and-kandarian-demons/1100-6503469/)].
- Good at: fear before it happens, and the shock for the teammates.
- Fails: the player who turned is no longer the one acting [read].

### 2. A clock on my body, then I play the monster
- Resident Evil Outbreak: every player has a virus gauge that rises and rises faster with damage; online, at 100% the player turns into a zombie they control and can attack former teammates for a short time [source: [Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biohazard_Outbreak), [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/resident-evil-outbreak-new-details/1100-6027102/)].
- Zombie Panic! Source: one zombie has a chance to infect on each hit; the infected survivor turns after about a minute, is blocked from voice, text and voice commands so they can't tell the team, and becomes open to friendly fire so teammates can kill them first; after turning they play on the zombie side [source: [Zombie Panic wiki](https://wiki.zombiepanicsource.com/Zombie), [older Fandom wiki](https://zombiepanic.fandom.com/wiki/Infection?oldid=8267)].
  The wiki frames the minute before turning as a choice: help the survivors, or use the time to hinder them [source, Fandom wiki].
- Good at: both halves exist in shipped multiplayer games, a feared clock before and a player-controlled monster after.
- Fails: after the turn the player is on the other side with a real win, so it is a side switch [read].

### 3. Do it yourself now, or lose control
- World of Warcraft, Blood-Queen Lana'thel: a bitten player gets a large damage boost, then a thirst; they must bite another player within 10 to 15 seconds or they are mind-controlled and attack the raid with doubled damage; biting cures the thirst and makes the bitten player a vampire too [source: [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/wotlk/npc=37955/blood-queen-lanathel), [Warcraft wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Blood-Queen_Lana%27thel_(tactics))].
- Good at: the player stays in control and still has to hurt a friend; standing still is the worst move, because it hands the body to the game.
- Fails as a source of fear: in the raid the bite is planned and wanted, since it spreads the damage boost [read].
- Reception (searched 2026-11-17): only strategy guides turned up, no player opinions on how the bite feels.
  Guides tell raids to follow an assigned bite order, the vampires double every minute until nobody is left to bite, and one guide says an addon that names each player's bite target "makes the fight the easiest end-tier boss in the raid" [source: [Icy Veins](https://icy-veins.com/wotlk-classic/blood-queen-lana-thel-encounter-guide-strategy-abilities-loot), [Warcraft Tavern](https://warcrafttavern.com/wotlk/guides/Blood-Queen-Lanathel)].
  So the one documented player response is turning the choice into a list [read].
- The game around it is a hit (12 million subscribers announced on 7 October 2010 [source: [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/-i-world-of-warcraft-i-reaches-12-million-subscribers-worldwide)]), which says nothing about this one boss among hundreds [opinion].

### 4. Pushing for power risks a forced act
- Alien: The Roleplaying Game: stress adds dice to every roll, and a 1 on a stress die forces a panic roll that can end in freezing, fleeing, going berserk or firing on comrades; pushing a failed roll adds stress [source: [Tabletop Gaming](https://tabletopgaming.co.uk/features/talking-alien-the-roleplaying-game-with-free-league-publishing), [EN World](https://www.enworld.org/threads/game-design-masterclass-alien.670332/)].
  A player complains that attacking an ally only because they are near "doesn't quite make sense" [source: r/alienrpg, via search summary].
- Darkest Dungeon: a stressed hero gets an affliction and acts on their own (takes treasure, refuses healing, attacks); the designers wanted heroes that react to stress like people, and said balancing a system that takes agency away was hard [source: [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-i-darkest-dungeon-s-i-affliction-system), [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/how-darkest-dungeon-got-inspiration-from-band-of-brothers-and-aliens)].
- Good at: the forced act comes from the player's own earlier push, so they own it.
- Fails: it needs something worth pushing for, and a forced act with no visible cause reads as arbitrary [read].

### 5. The game hands the player a script
- Vampire: The Masquerade: a frenzied vampire is run by the Storyteller, or the player "rides the wave", gives in on purpose and plays the frenzy out themselves (fan references disagree on the details) [source: [Chicago by Night reference](https://chicago-by-night.pages.dev/reference/v20/frenzy)].
- Monsterhearts: every character type has a "Darkest Self", a worst version the player must play until an escape condition is met [source: [Wikipedia](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Monsterhearts)].
  The werewolf's escape condition (wounding someone you care about, or sunrise) is from memory; the search didn't find the text (memory).
- Good at: the player does the acting.
- Fails outside a tabletop: nobody is there to hold the player to the script [opinion].

## What makes taking control away acceptable
- It fits when control is one of the game's themes, and it feels fair when it has known rules and a time limit [source: [Nomad's Reviews](https://www.nomadsreviews.co.uk/post/design-sins-taking-control-away-from-the-player)].
- A risk the player accepted beforehand doesn't count as lost agency [source: [EN World thread](https://www.enworld.org/threads/game-mechanics-and-player-agency.665238/)].
- Agency is a feeling the game provokes, not a fixed property of the rules [source: [Critpoints](https://critpoints.net/2025/09/16/player-agency-doesnt-make-sense)].

## Not found
- No game where the body pulls toward teammates and the player's inputs hold it back.
- No werewolf game where the turned player lacks control: Wolfsbane and Far From The Darkness give the werewolf player full control and a win of their own [source: [Epic Games Store](https://store.epicgames.com/p/wolfsbane-c8bb3a), [VGDB](https://www.vgdb.co/games/far-from-the-darkness)].
- "Who Infected?" (Steam) is described in search results as "a player loses control of their actions and starts attacking teammates unwillingly", but its store page is empty and unreleased, so nothing is confirmed [source: [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3425330)].
