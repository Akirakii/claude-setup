# Proposal for the critic: Dome Salvage

**Fantasy:** A submarine crew docks at abandoned weather-controlled domes on the sea floor and carries loot and food out of the facility inside each one to feed a creature that lives in a hole in their own submarine.
One-player test: The line still works with one player, and a tool that sucks up small loot lets one player carry a crew's worth.

**Players:** 1–4, one crew. **A session:** one dome visit runs on a mission timer (length not found); a run is counted in days survived and has no ending (visits per sitting not found).

**Feelings the game is known for**
- "My light ran out and I was still looking for the way in."
- "It touched me once and I was dead."
- "I died early and sat there waiting for you lot to finish."
- "We fed him to the thing in the hole."

**The rules, one sentence each**
- The crew docks the submarine at a dome, walks through the dome's outdoor area, and has to find the entrance to a facility with generated rooms.
- Loot and food from the facility are carried back and fed to the creature in the submarine, and the target is counted in calories.
- The target must be met by a deadline (number of days not found), and what happens on a miss was (not found) beyond the run being scored by days survived.
- A mission timer limits each visit.
- A player carries four items.
- A hand tool that is both a light and a vacuum sucks up smaller loot so a player can carry more than four items, and it comes in a regular and a stronger kind.
- A light runs out of charge.
- Some doors and maps are locked and unlocked with Morse code patterns, and codes vary in length.
- Most creatures kill a player in one hit, or leave them at one health.
- Some objects in the world kill too: a microphone a player can speak into has vaporised a player.
- A dead player waits until the living finish the visit, then rejoins.
- A dead teammate's body can be picked up and kept, and players feed bodies to the creature in the hole.
- Merchants sell items, including weapons described as weak (number of merchants, stock and prices not found).
- Harder domes hold better gear, such as powered armour, and it is lost on death.

**How much there is:** 3 dome climates named (desert, dark forest, frozen tundra), with generated facility layouts that change with depth; 15 creature kinds according to one player guide, including robots; tools and items (count not found; a light, the vacuum light in two grades, weapons, armour and record players are named); merchants (more than one, count not found); upgrades (not found).

**Voice:** The game has voice chat between players (whether it fades by distance was not found), and a microphone object in the world takes speech; what else uses voice was (not found).

**The choices**
- Split up to cover the facility before the timer ends, or stay together because one hit kills?
- Keep searching for the entrance in the dark, or go back with what the outdoor area gave?
- Go to a harder dome for better gear, or stay on an easier one?
- Carry a dead friend's body back, or use the slots for loot?
- (A fifth choice was not found.)

**What the other end decides in the same second**
- Split or stay together: each other player makes the same choice for themselves.
- Keep searching or go back: nothing.
- Harder dome or easier: nothing.
- Body or loot: nothing.

**Scene, 4 friends** (put together from player reviews; no single well-known moment was found)
- The submarine docks and four players walk into a pitch-black dome looking for the facility entrance.
- They split up because of the timer, and one finds a door that needs a Morse code pattern.
- Inside, one player's light runs out of charge while they are vacuuming small loot.
- A robot reaches a second player and kills them in one hit, taking their head off.
- The dead player waits and watches while the others keep looting.
- A third player carries the body back to the submarine.
- The crew drops loot, food and the body into the hole and checks the calorie count.

**What experienced crews do:** Crews split up because of the mission timer, and solo players rely on the vacuum light to carry enough; nothing else was found, because the player guides could not be opened.
