- **A move that only acts on a teammate's body collapses at the receiving end.** Six of eight ideas make "move a teammate's body" the hub verb: throw, hook, slap, bounce, grab, haul. Wherever the receiver's answer has a dominant choice (stay limp, take, jump, stop, hold), that end turns into a puck by round 5, and P4 drops to ~ or ✗. Only R304, where both players fly and both must press, kept both ends deciding. Zero-sum body rules (R305 rope, R304 momentum) also need a stated way for the crew to make net progress, or they strand or soft-lock a player.
- **R fails on threats a habit can answer.** "Crack whoever braced longest" is answered by taking turns as anchor; "footsteps with 3 s warning" by flopping down; "random gaps" by "boost the bag carrier". Random positions change *where*, not *which plan*. No idea reached R ✓. R304 reached ~ only because its threat can't be steered and private crates push back. R306 and R307 share a core: the giver stops dead and the receiver gets the sum.
