# Proposal for the critic: Moonsick

**Fantasy:** It is full moon in a mansion with friends, and each of us fears turning into a werewolf that will hurt the others; when the moon touches me I have ten seconds for my friends to reach me, and if they don't, it is still me in there and I can't stop.
One-player test: Alone nobody can hold me through the fit and nobody can bring me back; the first beam that touches me ends the night. The line doesn't work with one player.

**Players:** 3–6, one crew. **A session:** one night is about 8 minutes; a run is night after night until a night fails; a sitting holds 4–8 nights.

**Feelings the game is known for** (not played yet; the feelings aimed at)
"It's me, it's me, it's ME, get over here!"
"I can't, I'm holding Ben!"
"One more bloom, the beam's turning away—"
"MOVE, I can't turn away from you!" "Sorry, sorry, sorry."

**The rules, one sentence each**
At dawn the crew drinks the silver cure, and it works only if the cauldron holds the night's mark of blooms and every friend is human.
Moonbeams four steps wide cross the courtyard at running speed, where everyone can see them; where the next one starts and when it swerves is not known, and they come every 20 to 40 seconds early in the night and every 8 to 15 seconds late.
Silver blooms open in the ground a beam has just crossed and close again after 6 seconds, so a bloom is picked close behind a moving beam.
A friend the beam touches for 1 second starts a fit: their hands shake and drop what they hold, and after 10 seconds they turn.
Two friends who hold a friend in a fit, both hands each, for 5 seconds end the fit, and a friend whose fit was ended sees for the next minute where each beam will start before it does.
A turned friend is the wolf and plays it, but it never stops running; they can only steer.
The wolf is pulled toward the nearest friend, a little at first and more every second, and after 20 seconds it can no longer turn away.
Whatever the wolf hits head-on it bites, and a bitten friend drops what they carry and starts a fit of their own.
The wolf tramples every bloom it runs over.
Two friends who grab the wolf from the side or behind and hold it for 5 seconds turn it back.
Holding takes both hands, so a holder has put their blooms down, and blooms on the ground are trampled like any others.
The mark rises every night, and a night that fails ends the run.

**How much there is:** one mansion courtyard with a doorway to the cauldron, no creatures other than the friends, one kind of bloom, no items, no shop.

**Voice:** none in the game; the friends are on a voice app where everyone hears everyone.

**The choices**
The beam is swerving and three blooms are open one step behind it: step in for them, or stay two steps back and pick one?
Ana's fit has started across the courtyard and I carry six blooms: put them down where the next beam may bring a wolf over them and run to hold her, or carry them to the cauldron first?
Two fits started at once and there are only two of us free: hold Ben, hold Cy, or split and save neither?
I am in a fit and one friend has reached me: stay here for the second, or run to the far wall so that if I turn I start as far from them as I can?
I am the wolf at 15 seconds: steer side-on at the two friends waiting to grab me, with one pass left before I can't turn, or run the long way round the beds?

**What the other end decides in the same second**
The friend in the fit runs toward their friends to be held, or away from them.
The second holder comes, or stays with the blooms and the beam.
The wolf steers its pull past its friends side-on, or misjudges and bites one.
The friend who can see the next beam calls it, or is busy holding.

**Scene, 4 friends**
- A beam crosses the roses; Ana and Ben pick close behind it; Cy carries blooms to the doorway; Dee watches the far wall.
- The beam swerves back; it touches Ana for a second; her blooms fall and she shouts "it's me".
- Ben puts his blooms down and holds her; a second holder is needed; Dee runs in from the far wall and Cy is in the doorway with his hands full.
- Dee arrives at 8 seconds; the hold needs 5; Ana turns in their arms and both are thrown off.
- Ana can't stop; she steers away, flattens Ben's blooms on the ground, and feels the pull toward Dee grow.
- Cy drops his blooms in the cauldron and comes out; Ana shouts "coming past the fountain, left side"; Cy and Ben catch her side-on at 17 seconds and hold.
- At second 3 of the hold a new beam starts at the fountain, straight at the three of them, and Dee, who never got her minute of sight, didn't see it coming.

**What experienced crews do:** (not played; Claude's guess) pick in pairs so a second holder is always two steps away, keep one friend with moon-sight calling beams, and bank blooms often.
**What changes it from round to round:** where and when the beams start and swerve, and how many the mark needs.
**What carries from one round to the next:** the rising mark only.
**Can a new crew lose? Does it get harder?** Yes: a wolf at dawn or a cauldron under the mark ends the run. Beams come faster through the night and the mark rises until pairs who stay two steps back can't reach it.
