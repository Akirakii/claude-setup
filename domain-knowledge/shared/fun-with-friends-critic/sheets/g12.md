# Proposal for the critic: Long Way Home

**Fantasy:** A group of friends at the end of a camping trip must drive one old camper van out of a wild valley to the highway.
One-player test: The line still works alone and the game can be started alone, but fan guides say the winch and reviving need a second person and a solo run is close to unwinnable.

**Players:** 1–4, one crew, online with invited friends only. **A session:** one run is the whole drive out of the valley; sources disagree on its length (one fan page says 6–7 hours, another says 60–90 minutes for four players), and the game autosaves at checkpoints so a run can be spread over sittings.

**Feelings the game is known for** (2–4, each as a line a player says to a friend)
- (not found in the sources as player quotes; the lines below restate what the rules produce.)
- "Stop, stop, stop, you're tipping it over!"
- "I hooked the cable to the wrong tree and pulled the van off the road."
- "A bear was hitting the van and none of us wanted to get out."

**The rules, one sentence each** (every rule a player must know; as many as the game really has, 8–14)
- The whole crew shares one rear-wheel-drive camper van, and one player drives it over rough physics-based terrain.
- The other players ride inside or walk outside to guide the driver, gather items, and fix the van.
- The van has one winch at the front and one at the back; a player carries the hook on foot, attaches it to a solid object, and the cable is then pulled in with a remote control kept in the van.
- The van's body takes damage and is repaired with a hammer and scrap metal, each piece restoring about a tenth of the body bar.
- The engine needs motor oil, which is scarce, and a warning light shows when it must be added.
- A damaged tyre is replaced with a spare using a drill.
- Players have health, restored by eating a burger made by cooking a raw patty on a one-use grill.
- A cigar raises stamina and a beer removes thirst.
- Snakes cannot be attacked; they follow thrown objects, and a bite needs an antidote, found at checkpoints.
- Bears hurt players and damage the van, and a can of bear spray scares one off.
- Eagles lift players into the air; they cannot enter the van, and a player clipped to the winch cable or caught by a teammate is safe.
- Burns are treated with a lotion found at random places.
- A downed player is revived with an adrenaline injector or comes back at a tombstone at the next checkpoint.
- The game saves only at checkpoints, which are fuel stations, and a van that is stuck or flipped beyond recovery means reloading the last one.

**How much there is:** One hand-made fixed map with 16 or 17 checkpoints; three hostile animal kinds seen in sources (snake, bear, eagle); one vehicle; about a dozen item kinds seen in sources (hammer, scrap, oil, drill, spare tyre, grill, patty, cigar, beer, antidote, lotion, injector, bear spray); hats as cosmetics; no upgrades or difficulty settings found; whether the van uses fuel is (not found).

**Voice:** Voice by distance; the crew uses it to guide the driver from outside the van and to call the start and stop of a winch pull.

**The choices** (3–5 lines a player really faces, "do X, or do Y?")
- Drive the slope straight, or stop and set up the winch?
- Hook the cable to this anchor, or walk further to a better angle?
- Get out to gather scrap and food, or stay in the van where animals cannot reach?
- Use the oil or the injector now, or keep it for later?
- Try to right the flipped van with the winch, or reload the last checkpoint?

**What the other end decides in the same second** (per choice, or "nothing")
- Slope: the players outside decide whether to tell the driver to go, turn, or stop.
- Anchor: the player on the remote decides when to pull and when to let go; the driver decides whether to add throttle.
- Getting out: the driver decides whether to wait or keep rolling.
- Scarce items: whoever holds the item decides; the others can only ask.
- Flipped van: the whole crew agrees or argues; nothing is decided by one end alone.

**Scene, 4 friends** (5–7 bullets, one real moment the game is known for, second by second)
- The van stops at the foot of a steep rocky rise it cannot climb on its own wheels.
- One player carries the front hook uphill and attaches it to a tree off to one side.
- A second player holds the remote, a third stands outside watching the wheels, and the driver waits.
- The remote player starts the pull without saying so, and the van swings sideways toward the anchor.
- The spotter shouts to stop as the van tilts, and the driver steers the wrong way.
- The van rolls onto its side, and the crew argues whether to winch it upright or reload the fuel station.

**What experienced crews do:** They split into a driver, a navigator, an item collector and a winch-and-repair player, put a spotter outside for hard ground, call out every start and stop of a winch pull, use the front winch for climbing and the rear one for backing or controlling a descent, pull in short bursts while watching the tilt, pick up every piece of scrap, save oil until the warning light, check oil before leaving a checkpoint, and drive through every checkpoint so the game saves.
**What changes it from round to round:** The map is fixed; some items are placed at random; anything else is (not found).
**What carries from one round to the next:** The checkpoint save within a run; nothing found that carries from a finished run to a new one, and one fan page says there are no achievements.
**Can a new crew lose? Does it get harder?** A crew can be thrown back to its last checkpoint by a wrecked or stuck van or by all players going down (the exact fail rule is (not found)); there are no difficulty settings; whether later parts of the route are harder is (not found).
