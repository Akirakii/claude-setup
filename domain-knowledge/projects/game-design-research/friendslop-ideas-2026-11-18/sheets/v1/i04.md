# Proposal for the critic: Moonsick

**Fantasy:** It is full moon in a mansion with friends, and each of us fears turning into a werewolf that will hurt the others: it is still me in there, and I can't stop.
One-player test: Alone there is nobody to hurt and nobody to hold me and bring me back; the line doesn't work with one player.

**Players:** 3–6, one crew. **A session:** one night is about 10 minutes; a run is night after night until a mark is missed; a sitting holds 3–6 nights.

**Feelings the game is known for** (not played yet; the feelings aimed at)
"It's creeping up my arms. One more flower."
"MOVE, MOVE, I can't stop!"
"Sorry, sorry, sorry, I tried to turn."
"Catch me at the fountain, I'm coming round on your left."

**The rules, one sentence each**
The silver cure in the cauldron is made from moonflowers, which open only where moonlight falls, in the courtyard and in rooms a moonbeam crosses.
Moonlight on you grows your fur, shade shrinks it slowly, and you see it on your own arms.
The more fur you have, the more you can smell: at half fur you see the rare silver blooms, worth three flowers, glowing through the hedges.
Clouds cover and uncover the moon without warning, so the whole courtyard can light up in one second.
At full fur you turn: you are the wolf and you play it, but it never stops running; you can only steer.
The wolf is faster than a friend, and whatever it hits head-on it bites.
A bitten friend drops what they carry and gains half a body of fur, so a second bite turns them.
The wolf tramples every flowerbed it runs through.
A wolf in moonlight grows, getting faster, and after a minute in the light it breaks through doors.
Two friends who grab the wolf from the side or behind hold it, and it is held as long as both keep both hands on it.
A flask of cure poured on a held wolf turns it back with no fur; a flask is filled at the cauldron before it is needed, lowers the cauldron, and takes one hand to carry.
The cauldron must reach the night's mark by dawn; the mark rises every night; a missed mark ends the run, and so does every friend being a wolf at once.
A friend who is still a wolf at dawn starts the next night with half fur.

**How much there is:** one mansion with a courtyard, no creatures other than the friends, one kind of flower in two worths, one item (the flask), no shop.

**Voice:** none in the game; the friends are on a voice app where everyone hears everyone.

**The choices**
My fur is at three quarters and a silver bloom is ten steps into the light: take it, or walk back to the shade?
A cloud is thinning and Ben is deep in the courtyard: run out to carry his flowers in, or stay in the doorway?
Ana has turned: do I put my flowers down and go to hold her, or get them to the cauldron first while she tramples the beds?
I hold a flask and one hand is free: fill the second hand with flowers, or keep it to grab a wolf?
As the wolf: steer wide around the beds and my friends and stay in the light, growing, or cut through the narrow hall where two of them wait and may be in front of me?

**What the other end decides in the same second**
The wolf steers to pass its friends side-on so they can grab, or misjudges and meets one head-on.
The two holders choose who lets go a hand to pour if neither carries a flask.
The friend in the doorway chooses to shut the door on the wolf or leave it open as the place to catch it.
The friend with the most fur decides whether to be a holder, since a bite would turn them.

**Scene, 4 friends**
- Ana, at three quarters fur, sees a silver bloom past the fountain and goes for it; Ben is picking common flowers beside her.
- The cloud goes; the courtyard lights up; Ana's fur fills before she reaches the shade and she turns.
- Ana can't stop: she steers hard left to miss Ben, clips the rose bed and flattens six flowers.
- Cy and Dee come out of the hall; Dee carries the only flask; Cy drops his flowers to have both hands.
- Ana shouts "coming round the fountain, on your left" and steers to pass them side-on; Cy grabs, Dee can only grab one-handed, and the hold fails.
- Ana's next lap is faster; Ben, bitten once already, stays in the doorway and won't come out.
- Dee hands the flask to Ben, grabs with both hands on the third lap with Cy, and Ben pours from the door.

**What experienced crews do:** (not played; Claude's guess) one friend at half fur picks the silver blooms, two stay low and near with a flask ready, and the picker is swapped when a cloud thins.
**What changes it from round to round:** when the clouds open, where the blooms are, and who starts with half fur.
**What carries from one round to the next:** the rising mark, and half fur on anyone left a wolf at dawn.
**Can a new crew lose? Does it get harder?** Yes: every friend a wolf, or a missed mark. The mark rises until common flowers picked at low fur can't reach it.
