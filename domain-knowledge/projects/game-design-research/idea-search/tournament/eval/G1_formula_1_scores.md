## Patterns

- **Most common ~ on F3: one end of the coupling only obeys or holds.** One-way chains (G104, G107, G135), anchor/leapfrog cores (G119, G147, G155), the passive one being shoved (G129) and a bottom that only follows the top (G157) all have an end that is a pressure plate. Fully visible, solvable states (G141, G153, G165, G113/G167) invite a quarterback.
- **Hard fails come from three things:** rule bloat (G101, G133 stack a traitor or private stake plus 3+ extras on the hub); one shared job where players only get in each other's way (G106); and a shortcut that lets one fast carrier clear the level (G127's door at the goal).
- **F6 and F7 are the batch's soft spots.** Information hidden by the game that no player wants to hide gets ~ every time (G104, G109, G112, G117, G137, G139, G157, G171); only a traitor or a private stake earns ✓. Most ideas leave the arena source unstated, so F7 rests on "talk" alone.
- **What the best share** (G110, G112, G143, G149, G151, G161, G169): each player's action lands on one specific friend and fails without that friend's own decision at the same moment; the action differs by position (joint, level, spot in line), and physics or one spawn rule carries the content. They lose points only on F7.
- **Heavy duplication:** G112 = G137 (steer the next body in a ring), G113 = G167 (a ring of players fencing a herd), G104 = G135 (a beam relayed by player-held mirrors), G117/G139/G171 (others see what you can't), the anchor-leapfrog family G119/G147/G155/G157, and the "dismantle what you stand on" family G103/G106/G169.
