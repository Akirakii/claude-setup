Friendslop here means: tagged Online Co-Op, plus a chaos tag (Funny, Comedy, Party Game, Party, Physics or Social Deduction) in its top 10 tags, and no big-game tag (survival, open world, RPG, souls-like, MMO, strategy, city or colony builder) in its top 5. Candidates come from Steam’s most-wishlisted, popular-upcoming and popular-new lists for Online Co-Op plus each chaos tag. Wishlist rank is the game’s best position in those most-wishlisted lists (each tag list ranks separately, so several games can be #1). Live player counts are a single snapshot. Many follower counts are missing because Steam rate-limited the requests.

Summary

What the scan shows

  1. 1

    About 370 upcoming friendslop games are on Steam’s radar, but only a handful have real pull. The busiest demos right now: Worst Class Ever (~12k playing), Fart Together (~6.7k), Dumpster Gang (~1.6k).

  2. 2

    The biggest recent releases are old hits. R.E.P.O., PEAK, How to Fish, RV There Yet? and Gamble With Your Friends still lead on live players; new games compete with them for the same evenings.

  3. 3

    CRACK is the breakout of the last week: a $6.79 co-op incremental mystery about cracking a giant egg, ~6.8k playing five days after launch.

  4. 4

    A school-cheating microtrend: Finals Week (out, ~2.6k playing), CheatCheat (upcoming) and Worst Class Ever (Nov 9).

  5. 5

    No drill-to-the-core friendslop is on the lists. Same-premise games outside these lists are tiny (Drill Crew, BORE to the CORE).
