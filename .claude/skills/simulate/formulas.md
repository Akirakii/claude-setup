# Formulas each scene is checked against

## Bluff (researched 2026-11-14; sources in Moonsick `docs/bluff-research.md`)
A lie works only when every line passes, for each kind of lie in the design:
1. **Only one knows:** the liar holds a fact the others can't see.
2. **A reason to lie:** the liar gains from it in some moments.
3. **Caught costs:** a lie found out costs the liar.
4. **A wrong call costs:** accusing an honest player costs the accuser.
5. **Checking costs:** a claim can be checked, but not for free (time, an item, risk).
6. **Honest play isn't always best:** in some moments lying beats the truth, or nobody lies.
7. **Again and again:** the claim comes up many times in a game, not once (a single yes-or-no is not a bluff game).
