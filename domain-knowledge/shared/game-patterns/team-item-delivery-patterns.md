# Delivering an item as a team job

How games make carrying the right thing to the right place need more than one player.
Tags: [source] = said by the linked source; [read] = Claude's reading of it; [memory] = Claude's memory, not checked; [opinion] = Claude's own.

## The problem in general terms
A team must bring the right object to a person before a timer runs out, and each need has one obvious object.
How do you make the delivery a shared, risky and funny act, instead of one person's errand?

The fetch-quest trap is exactly this: "travel, collect, return" with no challenge [source: gamedeveloper "Gathering
Quests Sucks"; docseuss], a lock with no interesting key [source: jboger, Monkey Island quest lessons].

## Patterns
1. **Prep before use** (Overcooked). An item needs a step at a station before it works. Overcooked's core is "too
   many tasks for the number of players" [source: Road to the IGF, Duncan]; recipes were kept simple on purpose and
   the layout makes the difficulty [read: Road to the IGF; Develop post-mortem]. Fails: if one player can do the
   whole chain, it's just a longer errand [opinion]; recipe balance took most of their dev time [read].
2. **Big shared items** (Moving Out, R.E.P.O.). Some objects need two people to carry. Moving Out 2 reviewers say
   it "shines" in those segments [read: The National, Shacknews]; R.E.P.O.'s big loot needs coordination through
   narrow halls and doorways [read: game8, R.E.P.O. guides]. Fails: Moving Out's first hour is a free-for-all with
   only the odd couch [read: co-optimus/gideonsgaming], and it ships an assist that makes two-person items lighter
   [read: Moving Out pages], so small or new groups struggle. If one carrier leads and the other just follows, the
   follower only obeys [opinion, co-op check point 7].
3. **Fragile items** (R.E.P.O., Surgeon Simulator). Bumping or dropping an item breaks it or lowers its value [read:
   R.E.P.O. reviews: "slamming a fragile vase into a wall shatters it"]; Surgeon Simulator makes clumsiness the joke
   on purpose [read: Gaming Trend, Bossa interview]. Fails: deliberate clumsiness turns into frustration [read:
   Gamereactor]; a careful player can still do it alone [opinion].
4. **Heavy, two-handed items** (Lethal Company). Two-handed scrap blocks climbing and picking up more, and weight
   slows you [read: Lethal Company scrap guides]. Fails: alone, it's just slower [opinion].
