- **G stops most (13 of 33 first ✗; only R105 fails P).** Nearly every idea passes P because its rule aims at one friend. But by round 5 the crew's plan turns one end into a pressure plate: the friend who waits in cover (R106, R114), sits under a rock (R128), is a stepping stone (R140) or a donor (R148), the blind ones obeying a single seer (R101, R120, R154), the lifter or striker acting on a call (R107, R116), the passive ball being shoved (R109). R146 fails the other way: its speed-up rule makes one kiter carry it.
- **R stops 6 more (15 ✗ in total).** Three causes: (1) "help marks the friend" threats are cancelled by a safe spot (cover, rock, out of sight: R106, R128, R134); (2) threats that only follow the holder are tools the team steers, not pushback (R112, R120, R130); (3) "longest/most" targeting is answered by a fixed rotation (R101, R104, R124).
- **"Why the plan can't settle" is mostly geometry.** New spots and generated floors change only whether a plan works, not which plan is best. R got ✓ only where a reveal reassigns roles: dealt colours (R108), the shard spawning beside a random player (R138), leaks flipping who controls a hatch (R142), clues landing in different rooms (R144).
- **Private stakes (bets, cards, gems, dares) bring R up to ~, never further, and never fix G** (R111, R118, R122, R136, R146, R148). Five cores recur: hot potato with a hunter (R103/R120/R122/R130/R146), help that marks the friend as the target (R108/R128/R134/R152), hooks that only grab friends (R136/R138/R150), place swaps (R106/R114) and held doors (R112/R142). Lag risk clusters in catches, touches, shoves, swaps and braces read at landing.
- **Clean or near-clean:** R138 (all ✓); R108, R122 and R136 (one ~). R144 passes all gates but depends on in-game voice.
