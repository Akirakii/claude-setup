- **Taken most often: "split one known verb or view among players."** Platforms made live for a walker (G111), swapped cameras (G120), per-player trap visibility (G117), a carried beam (G135), a passed object (G144), trapeze timing (G162) and dogs with different abilities (G156) all exist as co-op games already. Usually it is a 2-player itch or Steam title, and this idea only adds seats.
- **Physical co-op carry or steer toys are crowded.** Raft weight (G105), a tethered balloon (G150), parachute ball (G114) and shared puppets (G153) all have close matches. The survivors (~) live only where every part has two owners, or where a traitor holds one edge.
- **"You can't see your own X" (G117, G132, G171) borrows Hanabi's information rule.** It stays ~ only when friends can also change your X. The only ✓ (G138 Mess Chain) chains each player's output into the next player's input, and no game was found that does this.
