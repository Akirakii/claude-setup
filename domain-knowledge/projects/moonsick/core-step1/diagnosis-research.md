## Moonsick against these [opinion]
- Moonsick stacks all three shapes at once: the patient feels signs, friends see signs, tools read signs, all
  combined through a % table. No game found does all three.
- It asks for the puzzle shape (combine clues) under the conditions the puzzle games avoid: the subject walks around
  in the dark, in first person, with 75 s on the clock, and with subtle signs.
- The real-time friend games (NMRiH2, The Thing, Zombie Panic) make *what it is* easy and put the drama in *tell or
  hide, test whom, cure in time, stop them before they turn*.
