# Proposal for the critic: Hold On

**Fantasy:** We rob the treasure niches of a deep well, and the only way down is each other: I hang from the rim, Ben hangs from my ankles, Cy from his, and we want one more handful before somebody's grip gives.
One-player test: Alone I reach only the niches at the rim, which hold almost nothing; the line doesn't work with one player.

**Players:** 3–6, one crew. **A session:** one night is about 8 minutes; a run is night after night until a quota is missed; a sitting holds 4–8 nights.

**Feelings the game is known for** (not played yet; the feelings aimed at)
"I'm slipping, drop the cup, DROP THE CUP."
"Don't let go, don't let go, don't—"
"Who let go?"
"Kick it off my hands!"

**The rules, one sentence each**
A friend can grab the rim, a ledge on the wall, or the ankles of a hanging friend, so a crew hangs as a chain from one friend at the top.
Everyone who is holding has a grip that drains faster the more weight hangs below them, friends and treasure together.
When a grip runs out that friend lets go, and they and everyone below them fall.
Grip refills only while standing on the rim or a ledge.
The friend at the bottom takes treasure from the niches, and treasure goes up hand to hand; passing uses one hand, and a one-handed grip drains twice as fast.
A friend can pocket what reaches them instead of passing it, which keeps its weight on everyone above them.
Any ledge can become the new top of the chain, so a crew can go deeper by climbing down over each other.
Whoever falls lands in the water at the bottom, loses what they held, and needs 30 seconds on the side ladder to get back to the rim.
Deeper niches hold richer treasure, and the niches are filled differently every night.
A well crab climbs the wall to one hanging friend at a time and pinches their hands, which doubles their drain until it is knocked off.
The pinched friend can swat it with one hand, or the friend below can kick it off, and a kick swings everyone below so the bottom friend can't grab for 3 seconds.
The water rises through the night and covers the lowest niches first.
The quota rises every night, and a missed quota ends the run.

**How much there is:** one well, generated niches, one kind of creature, about 6 kinds of treasure that differ only in weight and worth, no items, no shop.

**Voice:** none in the game; the friends are on a voice app where everyone hears everyone.

**The choices**
At the bottom: grab one more piece, which is weight on every friend above me, or call "up"?
In the middle: my grip is at a third and Ana below me holds the gold cup: tell her to drop it, pocket nothing and pass fast, or hold and hope?
The crab is on Ben's hands above me: kick it off and swing Ana away from the niche, or leave Ben to swat one-handed?
Who goes on top this time: the friend with the fullest grip, or the friend who is worst at the bottom?
Go deeper by making the ledge our new top, with a longer climb back, or stay shallow and take the cheap niches again?

**What the other end decides in the same second**
The friends above the bottom one say their grip out loud and decide whether to pass or pocket.
The friend below a failing grip drops the treasure, or climbs up over the failing friend to the ledge.
The pinched friend swats one-handed or waits for the kick.
The top friend lets go on purpose to save the treasure already on the rim from a chain that is going anyway, or holds.

**Scene, 4 friends**
- Ana holds the rim, Ben her ankles, Cy his, and Dee is at the bottom, three bodies deep, at a niche with a gold cup and two rings.
- Dee takes the rings and passes them up; Cy passes one-handed and his grip drains double for a second.
- The crab arrives on Ben's hands; Ben's drain doubles with three friends and a ring below him.
- Cy kicks the crab off; the swing pulls Dee away from the cup.
- Dee grabs again and takes the cup, the heaviest piece in the well; Ben says "I'm at a quarter".
- Ana on the rim has a full grip and can do nothing; Ben lets go.
- Ben, Cy and Dee fall past the niches into the water with the cup, and Ana holds two rings on the rim.

**What experienced crews do:** (not played; Claude's guess) the fullest grip in the middle, the lightest friend at the bottom, and a called number before every extra piece.
**What changes it from round to round:** where the rich niches are, how high the water is, and which friend the crab picks.
**What carries from one round to the next:** cash toward the rising quota, and nothing else.
**Can a new crew lose? Does it get harder?** Yes: a crew that falls twice misses the quota. The quota rises until the shallow niches can't pay it.
