# Proposal for the critic: Night Clearance

Target: online co-op for 3 to 6 friends who are on Discord, one developer, about 3 to 4 months, testable by 1 human plus bots, low content (one arena, one short list).

**Fantasy:** we are a broke clearance crew sent into abandoned, haunted factories to haul out junk for a company quota, and what lives inside takes whoever is alone, slow or loud.
One-player test: the line works with one worker, only worse. The need for a friend is not in the line; it comes from the rules below.
Does one player want and fear the same thing? Yes: more junk is deeper in, and deeper in is where the things are.

**Feelings, each about a friend**
1. "Where's Ben? He stopped talking": fear for a friend I can't see.
2. "One more room": I want another load, and the friends waiting on me pay for it.
3. "Get it off me!": I can't fix this and a friend can.
4. Laughing at Cy's scream, cut off mid-word.

**The rules, one sentence each**
1. The crew lands in the morning and the van leaves at midnight; anyone not in the van then is lost.
2. Junk lies through a building whose layout is new every visit; each piece has a value and a weight, weight slows the carrier, and a carrier holds one big piece in two hands or four small ones.
3. A quota in cash is due every third day and rises each time; miss it and the run is over.
4. If the whole crew is lost on a visit, everything loose in the van is lost with them.
5. Things come out as the day goes on, more of them and worse ones later, inside and outside; each kind has one rule.
6. Four of the kinds, to give the idea (about fifteen in all):
   - one drops from the ceiling onto a single head, blinds and chokes that player, and only a teammate's hit knocks it off;
   - one moves only while nobody is looking at it, fast;
   - one stalks from behind, backs off when glanced at and kills when stared at or ignored;
   - one is blind and hunts by sound, the players' own microphones included.
7. Voice inside the game carries only as far as a real voice would; radios are a thing the crew buys; a lost player can't speak to the living.
8. One player may stay in the van, where a screen shows the building's map with teammates and the things as dots; that player can work the building's doors, and can pull one teammate out at the price of everything that teammate carries.
9. A lost friend's body can be carried back, which lowers the fine for losing them.
10. Cash left over after the quota buys tools: lights, a shovel, radios, the pull-out machine.

A day lasts about eleven minutes. A first quota is met by roughly one good visit; later ones need three.

**The choices**
- It is six in the evening and the van holds half a quota: go back in for one more load, or leave with what we have?
- The thing that only moves unwatched is in the corridor: do I stand and stare at it so Ben and Cy can empty the room behind me, or do we all run?
- Cy went quiet two minutes ago: do I go and look for him, or keep hauling?
- Ana is in the van and sees a dot closing on Ben, who is carrying the best piece of the day: does she pull him out and lose the piece, or shout and hope?

**What the other end decides in the same second**
- Ben and Cy, behind the one who stares: empty the room fast and badly, or carefully, knowing a friend's eyes are the only thing holding the corridor.
- Cy, silent because his radio is with someone else: wait where he is, or walk toward where he thinks the door was.
- Ben, hearing Ana shout: drop the piece and run, or keep it and trust her doors.

**Scene, 4 friends**
- Ana takes the van. Ben, Cy and Dee go in with two lights and one shovel.
- Twenty rooms in, the ceiling thing lands on Dee. She can't see and has seconds. Ben has the shovel but is holding a big piece in both hands.
- Ben drops the piece, swings, hits Dee as well as the thing, and frees her. The noise brings the blind one.
- Ana, on the screen, sees the blind one's dot turn toward them and closes a door on it. She can't tell them which door: only Cy has a radio, and Cy is two corridors away.
- It is nine. They have half of what they need. Dee is hurt and wants out; Ben wants the big piece he dropped.

**A perfect crew's round:** the things arrive whatever the crew does, at times and places the crew can't schedule, so the staring, the hitting and the pull-out happen on good nights too.

**The plan by night 5:** "one in the van, the rest in pairs, a radio in each pair, out by seven."
What changes it night to night: the layout, which kinds of thing are in the building, where the first one shows up, the weather outside, and how far behind the quota the crew is.
