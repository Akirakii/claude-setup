## 8. Storm Sailors (from the idea pool, put through the new order after critic round 1)

This idea was not written in this run: it is the top-ranked core of the idea pool, where it already went through the older checks, scenes at 3 and 6 players and three long scenes.
What this run adds is the part it never had: the feelings, and the shipped games behind each one.

**Fantasy (from the pool, Claude's):** we are a crew keeping a ship on course through a storm, and every rope I haul swings a boom across a friend.

**The fantasy, checked first**
- Does one player want and fear the same thing? No: nobody wants to hit a friend, and hauling is what the job needs.
- The premise, searched: chaotic co-op boats are a crowded shelf (Shore, Mayday Vacay, Barely Afloat, Trash Sailors); none found where the crew's own rope work sweeps the deck. Different because the danger is a friend's work, not the sea.

**Job:** haul the ropes that keep the sails trimmed; they slip loose every few seconds, and the wind keeps changing which sails matter.

**The choice (from the pool):** haul now and knock flat any friend in the boom's path, who slides toward the low rail, or wait for them to duck, which stops their own hauling and lets their sail flap.

**The rules (from the pool):** each rope swings one boom through a fixed arc; anyone in the arc is knocked flat and slides toward the low rail; a crouched sailor lets the boom pass and can't haul; a sailor at the rail goes over unless a friend grabs them within 5 seconds.
The ropes are rigged in new places each storm.

**Between storms (from the pool's later work):** a sail that flaps for more than two seconds gets a rip, and rips stay from storm to storm; a ripped sail slips faster, so its rope is hauled more often and its boom swings more often.
In the first storm, waiting for a friend to duck costs a little canvas; by the fourth, every wait rips more, and the crew has to choose whose sail they let go.
The season is five storms; five rips tear a sail off.

**Scene, 3 friends (from the pool):** Ana hauls the mainsail and its boom swings across Ben at the jib rope; Ben ducks and his jib flaps; Cy shouts that the wind is turning.
**Scene, 6 friends (from the pool):** six ropes and six booms, the deck a maze of arcs, everyone shouting at once; by the fourth storm two ripped sails make two booms that never stop, and the crew argues over whose to let go.

**What each role gains and loses (added in this run)**
- The hauler gains a trimmed sail for the crew and pays with a friend on the deck, and with being the one everyone saw do it.
- The one in the arc keeps their feet by ducking and pays with their own sail.
- The grabber at the rail leaves their own rope, so the save costs a third sail.
- Next storm: "you never shout before you haul" is teasing; nobody gained by hurting anyone, so no grudge.

**The plan by storm 5 and by storm 20 (from the pool's long scenes):** "shout before you haul, and everyone ducks" costs every ducker their own rope while the sails keep slipping, so the calls keep colliding; a learned hauling order per wind was the hole at storm 20, fixed by rigging the ropes anew each storm.
