### R101 Sleepwalkers
- Rule (one sentence): Any player can sing a teammate to sleep; sleepers see the dream-house's hidden holes and the Nightmare but can't move, so awake players must carry them.
- Fantasy (who you are, what you're doing): Friends stuck in one shared dream, ringing the alarm clocks hidden in a dream-house to wake up.
- Aesthetic (one line): A soft pastel house with floating furniture; a sleeper's view flips to stark black and white, where the holes and the tall Nightmare show.
- Player on player (what my action does to a friend, and what they decide back): I sing Pia to sleep; she has 2 s to dodge if it is a bad moment. Now she sees the holes and must tell Max where to carry her, and Max can carry her into one. Anyone can slap her awake, which costs everyone the view.
- Why the plan can't settle (what changes round to round because of the players): The holes reshuffle whenever nobody is asleep, so someone must always be out, which means someone must always carry. The Nightmare walks toward whoever has slept longest, so the sleeper must change mid-round. The clocks are in new spots each round.
- Hub (the one shared thing everyone uses): The dream-house.
- At 3 / at 6: At 3 there is one sleeper, one carrier and one runner to the clocks. At 6 there are two of each.
- Hidden (what, how uncovered, why hide) or "none": The holes and the Nightmare, uncovered by being asleep. There is no reason to hide (the setting hides them).
- A failed run (what happens, who is blamed): Max carries Pia straight into a hole she called as "left" (his left or hers). Both fall. Pia's call and Max's step were both heard and seen.
- Extra rules (2): holes reshuffle when nobody sleeps; the Nightmare goes to the longest sleeper.
- Risk: the sleeper sees and the carrier acts, which is close to the Keep Talking split. A "rotate every 30 s" plan card may settle it (R ~).
- Novelty: the search found no match (Dream Eaters.exe and Somnum 2 are dream co-op games with other cores). Source moment: carrying a passed-out friend.
