# Fantasy-first ideas (2026-11-10 night)

User's ask: "generate ideas based on pure fantasy that you think is highly coupled with players. just that."
No rules, no checks: only the fantasy (who you are, what you're doing), chosen because the fiction itself
makes the players depend on each other. Written by Claude, unscored.

1. **Last Show:** a family of trapeze artists doing their final show while the big top burns; every trick
   ends in someone's hands.
2. **Shared Breath:** shipwrecked divers with one air hose between them, sinking with the wreck.
3. **Possession:** ghosts who can only act by slipping into a friend's body for a moment.
4. **Living Bridge:** acrobats crossing a collapsing city by turning each other into bridges, ladders and catapults.
5. **Pallbearers:** carrying a friend's coffin through a haunted town, and the friend inside isn't quite dead.
6. **Human Cannonballs:** a circus crew where each of you is the next one's ammunition.
7. **Coven:** witches whose every spell needs another witch's hand, voice or blood.
8. **Puppet Troupe:** marionettes on a stage, each one's strings held by another puppet.
9. **Avalanche Patrol:** a ski patrol digging each other out while the mountain keeps falling.
10. **Smugglers' Ball:** thieves passing one stolen jewel hand to hand across a masked ball full of guards.
11. **Sleepover Monster:** kids at a sleepover who are safe only while touching another kid when the lights go out.
12. **Night Swap:** a cursed house party where every night you wake up in a different friend's body.
13. **Vampire Family:** vampires who can only feed on each other, so someone always has to give.
14. **Storm Lifeguards:** lifeguards on a stormy beach who end up saving each other more than the swimmers.
