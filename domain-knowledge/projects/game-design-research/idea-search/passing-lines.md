1. **Sin Eaters**
   - Before: Pull sins out of the dead man's coffin and eat them. Each sin grows in you and makes you hit or grab whoever is near, so a friend eats part of it off you and carries it. Empty the coffin before dawn so his soul can rest.
   - After: Lift a sin, a black worm, out of the dead man's coffin and swallow it, and a dark stain starts spreading over your body. Wrath swings your arm at whoever stands closest and Greed locks your hand on whatever you touch, friends included; you can hold your arm back, but the stain spreads faster while you do. A friend who presses a crust of bread to your chest for three seconds draws half the stain into their own body. Swallow every worm before the rooster crows; a stain that covers you whole drags you into the coffin.
2. **Hiccup Spells**
   - Before: Brew the potion. Each ingredient needs a spell cast on it, and your hiccups are the only spells you have. The hiccups get faster and fire at whatever you face, friend or cauldron, and a friend can scare them out of you but then hiccups too. Finish the potion before the teacher returns.
   - After: Drop the ingredients into the cauldron, but the frog must be shrunk and the moon-ice frozen first, and your hiccups are the only spells you have. Each hiccup shoots a random bolt (shrink, freeze, float, swell) at whatever you face, cauldron or friend, faster and faster until a big one blows you across the room. A friend who jumps in front of you shouting "BOO" cures you, and the hiccups jump to them. Fill the cauldron before the teacher's footsteps reach the door.
3. **Burning Set**
   - Before: Set yourself on fire on purpose for each stunt shot. The fire spreads, and only a friend's tackle puts you out, which can light the tackler. Get every stunt on film before the reel runs out.
   - After: Light your own jacket and run the stunt past the camera: through the window, down the stairs, across the car roof. While you burn you can only run, never stop, and the flames climb from your sleeve to your head. A friend must dive onto you and roll you on the floor, and a spark can catch on their clothes. Get every stunt on the shot list before the reel runs out.
4. **Werewolf Pack** (cure-brewers)
   - Before: Stir the cure in the moonlight it needs, the same moonlight that turns you. A turning friend must be pinned down, and their scratches start turning the pinners. Finish the cure before the moon sets.
   - After: Stand in the moonbeam and stir the silver pot, and every second in the light grows fur up your arms. When fur reaches a friend's face they lunge at the nearest person, and two of you must pile on top of them until the fur falls away, while every claw that lands puts fur on your own arm. Pour the finished cure over each other before the moon dips below the trees.
5. **Cursed Masquerade** (actors)
   - Before: Perform the Duke's masked play, where every scene needs someone wearing a cursed mask. The mask takes over its wearer, and a friend can pull it off but then it sticks to them. Finish the play without losing an actor to a mask.
   - After: Put on the scene's mask and hit your marks while the Duke watches: the Jester mask for the juggling, the Knight mask for the sword fight. The mask makes your body play its part for real: the Jester trips whoever walks past, the Knight swings at the nearest actor. A friend must grab the mask with both hands and yank for two seconds, and it snaps onto their own face. Reach the final bow with every actor's face still their own.
6. **Haunted Tour** (ghost hunters)
   - Before: Get close to the ghosts to film them, which fills you with fear. Fear makes you see friends as monsters, and a friend's hug calms you but takes your fear. Film every ghost in the house before sunrise.
   - After: Walk your camera up to a ghost until it fills the frame, and every second that close makes your hands shake and your screen go grey. At full fear your screen paints your friends as monsters, and you run from them or swing your flashlight at them. A friend must wrap their arms around you and hold on while you flail, and your shaking passes into their hands. Get every ghost in the house on tape before first light hits the windows.
7. **Coven**
   - Before: Perform the ritual step by step, and every finished step calls down a curse. Only the witch the curse names can break it with her blood, which leaves her weaker for the next one. Finish the ritual and raise your coven's founder.
   - After: Light the next candle of the circle, and each lit candle calls down a curse: rats from the walls, fire on the robes, a bony hand from the floor. The curse whispers one witch's name, and it ends only when another witch cuts that witch's palm with the ritual knife over the flame. A cut witch moves and chants slower until dawn. Light all thirteen candles and the founder steps out of the fire.
8. **Possession**
   - Before: Do your unfinished business, which you can only touch from inside a body. You fade without one, and taking a friend's body pushes them out to fade. Every ghost finishes their business so you can all rest.
   - After: Each ghost has one thing to finish in the old house: post the unsent letter, wind the stopped clock, lock the cellar. You can only touch things from inside a body, and outside one your ghost goes more see-through every second. Dive into a friend's body to grab the key, and they're thrown out, fading until they dive into someone else. Finish every ghost's business before anyone fades away.
9. **Sleepwalkers** (dream thieves)
   - Before: Fall asleep on purpose to steal from dreams, while your sleeping body walks toward the fire. A friend must shake you awake in time, and the yawn spreads to them. Bring back every piece of the dream key before morning.
   - After: Lie down in the cursed bed to drop into the dream and grab a piece of the key, while your sleeping body gets up and walks toward the fireplace. A friend must grab your shoulders and shake you for two seconds to wake you, and each shake makes them yawn; a third yawn drops them asleep on the spot. Bring all seven key pieces back before the sun is up.
10. **Ice Queen's Kiss** (thieves)
    - Before: Steal the Ice Queen's jewels, and each one you touch freezes your heart. A frozen heart makes you shove friends away, and a friend's embrace thaws you but takes the frost. Steal every jewel before she wakes.
    - After: Pry a jewel from the Ice Queen's sleeping throne, and every jewel in your pocket spreads frost up your chest. Frost makes your arms shove away anyone who comes close, so a friend must get past your shoves and hug you for three seconds, and the frost crawls into their chest. Pry all twelve jewels before her eyes open.
11. **Vampire Family** (dinner party)
    - Before: Host the village dinner party without biting a guest. Hunger rises near them, only family blood eases it, and each course (garlic soup, silver forks) puts a new poison in your blood that you pass on with each bite. Get through every course before the guests leave.
    - After: Carry each course to the guests' table and smile, and every second near a guest's neck turns your eyes redder. A family member can hold out their wrist and let you drink for two seconds to clear your eyes, but you pass them whatever this course put in your blood: garlic makes them sneeze fire, silver makes their hands shake so they drop plates. Serve all five courses without a single guest bitten.
12. **Dancing Plague**
    - Before: Dance each afflicted townsperson down in the square. Dancing with them can give you the dance, and then only a friend can dance you down. Free the whole square before the bells of St. Vitus ring.
    - After: Take a dancing townsperson by both hands and match their steps, slower each beat, until they sit down on the cobbles. Every beat you hold hands, their feet may take yours; then you dance wild and can't let go of anything until a friend takes your hands and slows you down too. Sit every dancer in the square down before the bells of St. Vitus ring.
13. **Plague Doctors**
    - Before: Treat the town's sick, and every patient raises your own fever. You can't treat yourself, and a friend who treats you catches some of it. Treat every house before the plague cart comes.
    - After: Knock at a sick house and press leeches to the patient for three seconds, and each patient turns your own mask's glass redder with fever. You can't put leeches on yourself, so a friend presses them to you, and their glass reddens too. Clear every house with a red cross on its door before the plague cart rolls in.
14. **Berserker Clan**
    - Before: Rage up to fight the raiders, since rage is your strength. Too much rage turns you on friends, and a friend who holds you calm takes the rage. Drive the raiders off the beach.
    - After: Beat your shield and howl to fill your rage, then swing your axe at the raiders, harder the angrier you are. When your rage tops out your eyes go red and you swing at anyone, until a friend grabs you from behind and holds on for three seconds, and the red eyes pass to them. Push every raider back into the sea before their ships land more.
15. **Hypnotist's Show**
    - Before: Do the hypnotist's challenges on stage, and each one sends you under. The trance deepens, and a friend can snap you awake but may catch it through your eyes. Win the grand prize before the curtain falls.
    - After: Step up when the hypnotist calls you (stack the chairs, catch the falling egg), and each challenge starts with his watch swinging in your face. Under, you walk slowly toward the edge of the stage. A friend can stand in front of you and clap in your face to wake you, but if your eyes meet, the trance swings over to them. Win all his challenges before the curtain drops.
16. **Gremlin Hitchhikers**
    - Before: Patch whatever the gremlins break on the cargo plane. A gremlin on your back steers you, and a friend can swat it off but then it rides them. Land with the cargo intact.
    - After: Run to whatever is sparking (a fuel line, the radio, the left engine) and hold your wrench on it until it stops. A gremlin dropping from the vents lands on your back and yanks you toward the open cargo door. A friend smacks it with a rolled-up map, and it leaps onto their back. Keep every crate on board until the wheels touch down.
