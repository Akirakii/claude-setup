# Idea pool (living list; skill `generate-ideas`)

Every line is [job] [choice] [goal]. Claude writes and scores, so it isn't blind. Checks: the skill's `checks.md`.
Marks: ✓ pass · ~ weak · ✗ fail. Earlier versions of the pool: `pool-history/`. Older lines: `passing-lines.md`,
`fantasy1–3.md`.

**Choice shapes** (Shape column; added cycle 2, Claude):
- **P, push or pass:** keep the harm to make progress, or pass it to a friend / take it from one.
- **T, two ways to help:** two ways to save a friend, each costing something different. The usual form: take it
  onto yourself, or hurt them to end it.
- **U, use a friend:** use a friend's body, skill or sight and leave them worse off.
- **S, swap harms:** cure a friend's harm by giving them a different one.
- **W, where to put it:** choose which part of a friend (or of yourself) takes the harm, and so which ability is lost.
- **C, chain:** one friend's move frees the next friend to move (added cycle 6).

**Look** (added cycle 2): what the screen shows. It must show the state the choice depends on.

**Cores** (added cycle 4): ideas whose rule is the same are one core, told with different fantasies. The pool is
grouped by core. **26 passing ideas, 15 cores**, plus 5 skins (new fantasies on old cores).

## Rules (one sentence plus at most 2 extra rules, then the critic, checks 4.6)
- **Sin Eaters** (c2). Each worm adds one dealt sin to your stain (Wrath: you swing at whoever is nearest; Greed: you
  can't let go of what you hold; Sloth: you move at half speed); a friend pressing bread to you draws half of each sin
  into themselves. Extra: the coffin drags in anyone whose stain is full; two friends can pull them back out.
  Critic: a 3-line card ("call the sin; bread Greed and Sloth at once; for Wrath, back off and bread from behind");
  half-draws mix sins, so it bends. ~.
- **Coven** (c2). Each lit candle deals one curse that names one witch (rats slow whoever they touch; a fire ring
  closes on her); another witch's cut on her palm ends it but halves her speed until the next candle. Extra: caught
  by the fire ring = out. Critic: a 2-line card ("cut on fire; on rats, run"); the next candle may name the slowed
  witch again. ~.
- **Grave Robbers** (c2). A corpse grabs the nearest robber; pulling for 3 seconds frees them and the corpse grabs the
  puller; slamming the lid frees them at once and their rings drop back in. Extra: the lantern freezes anyone in its
  light. Critic: a 1-line card ("slam whoever holds the fewest rings"); pull chains and the lantern bend it. ~.
- **Werewolf Pack** (c3). The moonbeam grows fur; at full fur your wolf is pulled toward the nearest friend and swipes
  on its own while you fight the pull; two friends holding you 3 seconds turn you back; each swipe that lands moves
  fur onto the one it hits. Critic: a 2-line card; uneven scratches and a friend fighting the pull bend it. ~.
- **Midas Thieves** (c3). Every crate you touch turns to gold and adds gold to your arm; gripping a friend for
  2 seconds moves your gold into the part you grip (hand: can't pick up; foot: half speed; mouth: muted). Extra:
  gold at your shoulder = statue. Critic (c3): "always put it in the feet" may hold. Fixed c5: each night deals a
  mix of crates (hands), barrels (feet) and password chests (mouth), so the cheapest part changes. ~.
- **Sandstorm Tomb** (c4). Each gust comes from a dealt side and turns every bare limb facing it to dust (it stops
  working for the round); wrapping a friend's limb uses the linen from the same limb of yours, and nobody can wrap
  themselves. Extra: the door fills with sand over time. Critic: linen is one resource, so "share wraps evenly, keep
  hands bare" may hold; turning your wrapped side to the gust is a solo dodge. Fixed c5: a wrapped limb is stiff and
  slow, so wrapping a friend's digging hand protects it and slows them; they say which limb. ~.
- **Scrap Robots** (c4). Every few seconds one random part of one robot breaks (arm: can't weld; leg: can't climb;
  eye: can't see the holes); any robot can unbolt a working part from a friend standing still beside it and bolt it
  onto itself or another in 3 seconds. Extra: holes open at random and leak air until welded. Critic: "parts go to
  whoever is at the newest hole"; a robot who gave its leg can't climb to the next hole, so it bends. ~.
- **Helium Clowns** (c4). Each balloon adds helium to you (big ones more, shown by color); at the ceiling the fan pops
  you (you drop, lose your balloon animal, start at zero); hugging a floating friend for 2 seconds moves half their
  helium into you; a pin drops them to zero at once and bursts what they hold. Critic: the hug moves one resource, so
  "share evenly" may win; the pin is just an early fan. Fixed c5: a pin bursts all their helium into the balloon
  they hold and finishes it, and they lie dazed for 5 seconds; both sides of the choice now help the job. ~.
- **Living Dough Bakers** (c4). Kneading adds dough to your arms at the batch's speed; at full dough your arms are
  stuck; peeling a friend for 2 seconds moves all their dough onto you; the oven bakes it off at once but burns their
  hands, so they knead at half speed for 20 seconds. Critic: "peel when you're light, oven when you're heavy". ~.
  Same rule as Ice Queen's Kiss and Helium Clowns: core 1.
- **Plague Doctors** (c4). Each bed's patient has a fever and one of 3 sicknesses (cough: you drop what you hold;
  sweats: you slip when you turn; shakes: your aim wobbles); a fresh leech cures a bed and raises your own fever; a used
  leech carries its last sickness, and pressing it on a friend cures their fever but gives them that sickness. Extra:
  full fever = you collapse until a friend leeches you. Critic: who carries which sickness changes who can do what, so
