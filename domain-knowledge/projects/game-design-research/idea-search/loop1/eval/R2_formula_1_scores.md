- **The noise lure is the batch's default threat, and it always fails R5.** R207, R208, R215, R217 and R221 all use it, and R209's "goes for the just-lifted" is the same rule. "Goes to the last noise / ping / clang / lift" hunts whoever did X, so a decoy or a "stay far from it" line beats it. A "director paces it" note changes nothing. R221 passed every gate up to R and died only here.
- **Answer-with-two-options hand-offs settle into a request protocol by round 5.** The options are come/send, take/jump, take/grab, open/pass, catch/drop and burn/pass. The receiver asks and the giver obeys, or the answer is read off the scene, so P4 is the main cut. Five ideas failed it (R201, R204, R205, R215, R217) and four are weak on it (R202, R203, R211, R213). The P4 passes (R206, R208, R209, R221) are fresh spatial calls on every act: throw, push, lift, ping.
- **No idea passes R.** Random surroundings (waves, oil, clouds, respawns, rot reshuffle) change whether a plan works, not which plan. No threat targets the crew's habit, and the only private reason to break the plan (R205's saboteur) died at P. The best bases to repair are R221, then R208 and R206: their P, F and G are strong, so swapping the threat for one that answers the habit is the fix (R208 also carries lag risk).
