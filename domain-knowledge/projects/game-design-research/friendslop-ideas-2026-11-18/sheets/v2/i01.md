# Proposal for the critic: Gold Fever

**Fantasy:** We stole a king's cursed ring, so everything we touch turns to gold and the gold climbs our own bodies; only a friend's grip can take some of it off me, and we want to load one more crate before the ship or one of us goes under.
One-player test: Alone nobody can take my gold, so I am a statue after a few crates; the line doesn't work with one player.

**Players:** 3–6, one crew. **A session:** one night is about 10 minutes; a run is night after night until a quota is missed; a sitting holds 3–6 nights.

**Feelings the game is known for** (not played yet; the feelings aimed at)
"Take my arm, take my arm, it's at my neck!"
"I've got one hand left, I can't take any more of you."
"Cast off! Cast off NOW!" "One more crate."
"We're short. Sell Ben." "Ben is hopping away from the scale."

**The rules, one sentence each**
Touching junk in the ship's hold turns it to gold, and each touch adds gold to the hand that touched.
Gold on a body creeps on by itself, one part at a time: hand, arm, chest, the other arm and hand, each leg, then the head.
A golden part stops working: a golden hand can't grip or turn junk, a golden leg drags at half speed, two golden legs can't walk, and a golden head makes you a statue who can only hop.
Only a friend can take your gold: you grip one of their parts for 2 seconds and one part's worth of your gold moves into it; a part that is already gold takes no more, a statue takes none, and the friend can pull away.
Gold you made is light in your own hands and heavy in anyone else's: you carry your own crate alone, and anyone else's crate takes two carriers.
Golden junk counts only when it is carried up to the scale on deck.
Every golden thing brought aboard makes the ship sit lower, so water rises in the hold; in water above the knee everyone walks at half speed and a friend with a golden leg is stuck until pulled out, which takes 4 seconds and two working hands.
The crew chooses when to cast off: once everyone still walking pulls the mooring rope together, the night ends and the gold on the scale is banked.
If the water reaches the deck hatch before the crew casts off, the ship goes down and everything on the scale is lost.
A statue weighs as gold: carried to the scale and sold it is worth about a quarter of a night's quota, and that sum plus half again is added to the next night's quota to buy the friend back.
A friend who is a statue at dawn starts the next night with one golden hand; all other gold clears at dawn.
The quota rises every night, and a missed quota ends the run.
Each night the hold holds a different mix of crates, kick-along barrels and chests that take two touchers at once.

**How much there is:** one ship (a hold, a deck with the scale and the mooring rope), no creatures, 3 kinds of junk, no items, no shop.

**Voice:** none in the game; the friends are on a voice app where everyone hears everyone.

**The choices**
Touch one more crate and let the gold reach my shoulder, or carry up what I have already turned while my hands still work?
Ana grips me to hand over an arm's worth: do I give her my free hand, which ends my touching, or my leg, which will stick when the water comes?
The water is at the hatch's second step: pull the rope and bank what is on the scale, or go down for the chest two of us just turned?
Cy is stuck in the water with a golden leg: both hands on him for 4 seconds, or both hands on the crate only I can carry alone?
Ben is a statue and we are short by about his weight: sell him and pay for it tomorrow, or cast off short and end the run?

**What the other end decides in the same second**
The friend being gripped chooses which working part to give up, or pulls away.
The friends on deck hold the rope and wait, or let go and run down to help carry.
The stuck friend hands their last piece to a friend who can still walk, or holds it.
The statue hops toward the scale or away from it while two friends decide.

**Scene, 4 friends**
- Ana has turned four crates; her right arm is gold to the shoulder and creeping onto her chest; her crates are light only for her.
- She grips Ben; he gives his left leg; one arm's worth moves, her hand works again, and Ben drags.
- Ana carries her crates up one by one; the ship sits lower; the water in the hold reaches the knee and Ben's leg sticks.
- Cy and Dee have just turned a chest together and each has a golden hand; the chest takes both of them, with their good hands.
- Dee leaves the chest to pull Ben out for 4 seconds; Cy can't lift the chest alone and shouts from the hold.
- Ana is on deck with a hand on the rope; the water is one step under the hatch; the scale is a chest short of the quota.
- Ana lets go of the rope and runs down for the other side of the chest.

**What experienced crews do:** (not played; Claude's guess) everyone touches and carries their own, gold is handed into legs while the hold is dry and into spare hands once it floods, and one friend stays at the rope from the last third of the night.
**What changes it from round to round:** the mix of junk decides which parts are worth keeping, and the quota decides how low the ship must sit.
**What carries from one round to the next:** cash, the rising quota, the debt for a sold friend, and one golden hand on anyone who ended as a statue.
**Can a new crew lose? Does it get harder?** Yes: a sunk ship, everyone a statue, or a missed quota. A higher quota means a lower ship and less time before the hatch.
