# Novelty batch 2 (24 ideas)

### G102 Circle of Herds
- Rule (one sentence): Each player herds one flock that can only live by grazing on the next player's flock in a closed ring, and every flock must survive the season.
- Hub (the one shared thing everyone uses): the meadow and the flocks on it (one flocking-and-eating rule: touching your prey flock eats it, touching your predator's flock loses you animals).
- Coupling (how my best move depends on what a friend just did; who does which different action): I steer my flock toward my prey flock to feed, and away from my predator's flock. The owner of my prey decides whether to offer a few stragglers or hide, and my predator's owner decides whether to hold back. Each pair is a live two-way deal, and every player is predator to one friend and prey to another.
- Fantasy (who you are, what you're doing): shepherd spirits of a valley keeping the cycle of life turning through one hard winter.
- Look (one line): top-down watercolour meadow, each flock a coloured swarm, and frost creeping in from the edges.
- At 3 / at 6: at 3 it is a rock-paper-scissors ring, where one greedy flock starves the next within seconds. At 6 the ring has six links, but all six flocks share one shrinking meadow, so contacts nobody planned happen constantly.
- Hidden (what, how it's uncovered, why anyone hides it) or "none": none.
- A failed run (what happens, who is blamed): the green flock dies out and the ring collapses. Everyone saw purple's flock sit on green for ten seconds, or green's owner never let it graze.

### G105 Tipping Raft

Every move shifts the tilt, so the others must step to counter it or the raft swings into rocks. Where I should stand depends on where my friend just ran.

- Look (one line): top-down log raft in white water, crates bobbing past, a tilt arrow on the deck.
- At 3 / at 6: each person counts for 1/crew-size of the weight, so the tilt range stays the same. At 3 every step swings the raft; at 6 more jobs run at once and bodies crowd the deck. Rivers are generated.
- Hidden (what, how it's uncovered, why anyone hides it) or "none": none.
- A failed run (what happens, who is blamed): Mia dashed to the right edge for a crate, the raft swung into a rock and split. The whole crew saw her run and the tilt follow her.

- Coupling (how my best move depends on what a friend just did; who does which different action): I need you to tell me my colour, and the colour I need for the next door may be on someone I must bump. Each bump changes two players at once, so whatever I was told goes stale as soon as I touch someone.
- Fantasy (who you are, what you're doing): blindfolded thieves escaping a vault whose doors read the gem pinned on your back.
- Look (one line): top-down generated vault with coloured doors and glowing gems on players' backs.
- At 3 / at 6: at 3 there are few colours and every bump matters. At 6 there are more colours to track and more accidental bumps in corridors.
- Hidden (what, how it's uncovered, why anyone hides it) or "none": your own colour. You find it out by asking a friend. No player has a reason to hide it; the game hides it.
- A failed run (what happens, who is blamed): someone walks into the wrong door and sets off the alarm. Everyone heard who called the colour and saw who walked.
