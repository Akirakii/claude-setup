# Proposal for the critic: Moonsick, the wolf we need

**Fantasy:** It is full moon in a mansion with friends, and the silver we came for is behind doors only a werewolf can break, so one of us has to turn; I fear being the one who stays turned too long and stops being me among my friends.
One-player test: Alone, nobody can bring me back once I turn, so the first door is my last; the line doesn't work with one player.

**Players:** 3–6, one crew. **A session:** one night is about 10 minutes; a run is night after night until a quota is missed; a sitting holds 3–6 nights.

**Feelings the game is known for** (not played yet; the feelings aimed at)
"One more door. I've still got it. I've still got it."
"He's drifting toward you, get behind the table."
"Bring me back NOW, I can't turn away from you."
"Put the silver down and hold him!" "If I put it down he bites me!"

**The rules, one sentence each**
The mansion's silver is in rooms behind oak doors, and only a wolf can break an oak door; friends carry the silver out to the cart.
A friend turns by stepping into the moonbeam in the hall, and the moon reaches the hall only three times a night, for a few seconds each, whenever the clouds part; the crew doesn't choose when.
A fresh wolf is fully mine: I run, break doors and stop as I like.
Every door I break and every 20 seconds as a wolf adds one notch of hunger, and I see my own notches.
At each notch my wolf pulls harder toward the nearest friend: at 2 notches it drifts, at 4 I must keep steering away to hold a straight line, at 6 I can no longer turn away at all.
A wolf that hits a friend head-on bites, unless they hold large silver: the friend is knocked down for 5 seconds, and a friend bitten twice in one night turns, as a second wolf that starts at 5 notches and breaks no doors.
Silver burns a wolf: a wolf that runs into a friend who holds a large piece in both hands recoils and is dazed for 2 seconds, and nobody is bitten; small pieces don't stop it, and a friend with a large piece walks at half speed.
Hunger never falls while I am a wolf; only being turned back clears it.
Two friends with empty hands who grab the wolf from the side or behind and hold it for 3 seconds turn it back, so whoever holds has put their silver down first.
A wolf at 6 notches can only be held by three friends.
Which rooms hold the silver changes every night, and deeper rooms hold more behind more doors.
The crew chooses when to leave: once every friend is human and at the cart, the night ends and the silver on it is banked.
The cart can't leave a wolf behind: at dawn any wolf still loose runs the cart down and the night's silver is lost.
The cart's quota rises every night, and a missed quota ends the run.

**How much there is:** one mansion with generated silver rooms, no creatures other than the friends, one kind of loot in three sizes, no items, no shop.

**Voice:** none in the game; the friends are on a voice app where everyone hears everyone.

**The choices**
As the wolf at 3 notches with one door left before the big room: break it, or call my friends to bring me back now?
As a carrier with the wolf drifting at me: keep the silver up as my shield and walk on, or put it down so I can be one of the two who hold it?
As the crew: put two carriers' silver down to hold the wolf now, at 3 notches, or get the load to the cart first and need three of us later?
As the wolf at 5 notches: run for the far wing, away from everyone, where nobody can reach me before 6, or stay near my friends, where the only safe ones are those still carrying?
The clouds part for the second time tonight and four doors are still shut: do I step in, knowing I must break them all in one turn, or let the beam pass and hope for the third?

**What the other end decides in the same second**
The friends near the wolf choose to hold it now or keep carrying.
The carrier being drifted at keeps the silver between them and the wolf, or drops it to free their hands.
The wolf steers its pull into the friend who is carrying, to be dazed for the 2 seconds its friends need, or fails and hits the one with empty hands.
The friend bitten once already decides whether to be a holder, since a second bite turns them.

**Scene, 4 friends**
- Ana turns in the hall beam and breaks two doors; Ben, Cy and Dee carry silver from the first room.
- Ana is at 3 notches and drifting; the big room is one door further; she breaks it and is at 4.
- Ben and Cy run in for the big silver; Ana holds a line away from them by steering hard and says "come and get me after this load".
- Twenty seconds later she is at 5 and her wolf curves toward Dee, who is carrying alone in the corridor.
- Dee holds her silver up; Ana hits it, recoils and is dazed for 2 seconds.
- Ben and Cy drop the big silver in the doorway and run to hold her, but the 2 seconds are over: Ana, at 6 now, clips Cy, who is down with one bite.
- Three friends are needed and only Ben and Dee are standing; Dee puts her silver down, Cy gets up, and all three hold Ana with nothing in their hands and one bite between them and a second wolf.

**What experienced crews do:** (not played; Claude's guess) the friend nearest the hall takes each beam, breaks every door they can before 4 notches, and runs back toward two friends who have already put their silver down.
**What changes it from round to round:** where the silver rooms are, how many doors each needs, and who starts already bitten.
**What carries from one round to the next:** cash toward the rising quota, and nothing else.
**Can a new crew lose? Does it get harder?** Yes: a wolf loose at dawn loses the night's silver, and a missed quota ends the run. The quota rises until two doors per turn can't pay it.
