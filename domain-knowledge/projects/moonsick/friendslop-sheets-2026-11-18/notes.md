# Moonsick as a friendslop: two versions judged for fun (AFK run, 2026-11-18)

Asked by the user: make Moonsick work as a friendslop, keeping friends who survive the night and can turn werewolf, with turning as the fear; "moonsick A being the standard people are feared of transforming and need to help each other", and a Moonsick B.
The sheets and the critic's reports are in the game-design-research project, in its friendslop ideas run of the same date (the sheets `i04` for A and `i05` for B).
Nothing was built or played.

## Moonsick A (the standard), as it stands after five versions
- Fantasy (the user's lines, joined; the ten seconds are Claude's): full moon in a mansion with friends, each of us fears turning into a werewolf that will hurt the others; when the moon touches me I have ten seconds for my friends to reach me, and if they don't, it is still me in there and I can't stop.
- A moonbeam that touches me starts a 10-second fit; two friends holding me for 5 seconds end it.
- If they don't reach me I turn: I am the wolf, it never stops running, I only steer, it is pulled toward the nearest friend and after 20 seconds can't turn away; what it hits head-on it bites, and a bitten friend starts a fit.
- Two friends who grab the wolf from the side hold it back to human.
- The job: silver blooms open just behind a moving beam; a mark that rises every night; at dawn everyone must be human.
- The critic's verdict on fun with friends: maybe, five times, always just under the hits. A careful crew keeps its distance from the beam, so the turn rarely happens.

## Moonsick B (the wolf we need)
- The silver is behind doors only a wolf can break, so one friend turns on purpose when the moon reaches the hall; the wolf is fully mine at first and pulls harder toward friends with every door.
- Silver held in both hands stops a bite; holding the wolf needs empty hands.
- The critic's verdict: maybe. The crew waits for the three moments the moon reaches the hall, and the catch becomes a drill.

## What the five rounds showed (Claude)
- One reason ended every version: the crew can keep its distance from what turns them. In the hits the danger comes to the crew.
- What held in every version: the turned friend is the busiest player, and the catch needs the wolf's own steering and voice ("catch me on the left").
- Both versions turn a bitten friend instead of killing them, which softens the user's word "kill".
- The open choices are in the ideas run's questions file.
