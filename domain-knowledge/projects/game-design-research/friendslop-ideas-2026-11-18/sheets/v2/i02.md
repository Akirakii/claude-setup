# Proposal for the critic: Hold On

**Fantasy:** We rob the treasure niches of a deep well, and the only way down is each other: I hang from the rim, Ben hangs from my ankles, Cy from his, and we want one more handful before somebody's grip gives.
One-player test: Alone I reach only the niches at the rim, which hold almost nothing; the line doesn't work with one player.

**Players:** 3–6, one crew. **A session:** one night is about 8 minutes; a run is night after night until a quota is missed; a sitting holds 4–8 nights.

**Feelings the game is known for** (not played yet; the feelings aimed at)
"I'm slipping, drop the cup, DROP THE CUP."
"Don't let go, don't let go, don't—"
"Got you! ...you're heavy."
"Who let go?"

**The rules, one sentence each**
A friend can grab the rim, one of the few ledges on the wall, or the ankles of a hanging friend, so a crew hangs as a chain from one friend at the top.
Everyone who is holding has a grip that drains faster the more weight hangs below them, friends and treasure together.
Only you see your own grip, as your own hands shaking; the others know it only if you say it.
When a grip runs out that friend lets go, and they and everyone below them fall.
A hanging friend can catch a falling friend by the wrist as they pass, and a caught friend hangs from that arm as new weight on a one-handed grip, which drains twice as fast.
Grip refills only while standing on the rim or a ledge, and ledges are about four bodies apart.
The niches are in the smooth wall and can be reached only from the chain; the water below is a torrent that carries whoever falls in out through the drain, back to the rim 30 seconds later with empty hands.
The friend at the bottom takes treasure from the niches and it goes up hand to hand into a sack on the top friend's belt; passing uses one hand.
The sack is weight on the top friend's grip, and it is safe only once the top friend swings it onto the rim, which takes 3 seconds one-handed.
Any friend in the chain can shake off the friend holding their ankles, which drops everyone below and lightens everyone above.
A well crab climbs to one hanging friend at a time and pinches their hands, doubling their drain until the friend below kicks it off; a kick swings everyone below so the bottom friend can't grab for 3 seconds.
The water rises through the night and covers the lowest niches first; deeper niches hold richer treasure and are filled differently every night.
The quota rises every night, and a missed quota ends the run.

**How much there is:** one well, generated niches and ledges, one kind of creature, about 6 kinds of treasure that differ only in weight and worth, no items, no shop.

**Voice:** none in the game; the friends are on a voice app where everyone hears everyone.

**The choices**
At the bottom: grab one more piece, which is weight on every friend above me, or call "up"?
At the top: swing the sack onto the rim now, 3 seconds one-handed with everyone hanging from me, or keep both hands and keep collecting?
In the middle: my hands are shaking and three friends hang below me: say so and wait for them to climb, or shake them off and save the sack above?
A friend is falling past me: put out a hand and take their weight on one arm, or let them go to the water?
The crab is on Ben's hands above me: kick it off and swing Ana away from the niche, or leave Ben's grip to drain?

**What the other end decides in the same second**
The friends above say their grip or keep quiet to get one more piece up.
The friends below a shaking friend climb up over them toward the ledge, or drop what they hold to weigh less.
The falling friend throws what they hold to a friend still hanging, or keeps it and hopes for a wrist.
The top friend lets the whole chain go to save a full sack, or holds on with it.

**Scene, 4 friends**
- Ana holds the rim with the sack on her belt, Ben her ankles, Cy his, and Dee is at the bottom at a niche with a gold cup and two rings.
- Dee passes the rings up; they reach Ana's sack; Ana's hands start to shake and she says nothing, because the cup is next.
- The crab arrives on Ben's hands; Cy kicks it off, and the swing pulls Dee away from the cup.
- Dee grabs again and takes the cup; Ben says "I'm nearly gone".
- Ana starts swinging the sack onto the rim, one-handed, with three friends below her.
- Ben's grip goes; Cy and Dee fall with him; Dee throws the cup up and Ben, falling, misses it.
- Ana lands the sack with two rings on the rim, and three friends come out of the drain 30 seconds later.

**What experienced crews do:** (not played; Claude's guess) bank the sack after every rich piece, the lightest friend at the bottom, and a called word that means "I have ten seconds".
**What changes it from round to round:** where the rich niches and the ledges are, how high the water is, and which friend the crab picks.
**What carries from one round to the next:** cash toward the rising quota, and nothing else.
**Can a new crew lose? Does it get harder?** Yes: a crew that falls twice with a full sack misses the quota. The quota rises until the shallow niches can't pay it.
