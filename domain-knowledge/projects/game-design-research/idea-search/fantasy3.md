## Findings
- **Aiming at "the harm changes how you behave" worked:** 4 new-shape survivors (Masquerade, Hypnotist,
  Gremlins, Hiccups). But they are one core with Sin Eaters: *pass a behavior curse by helping*. A second attractor.
- **Every distinct core tried this round dropped** at the probe or the force check.
- **No fantasy in 3 runs reached ✓ on "no fixed plan"; every survivor is ~.** A fantasy can't say enough about
  what each round deals; only a rule can. The fantasy-first loop has reached its limit here: the next step is
  to write a one-sentence rule for the best survivors and check "no fixed plan" properly.
