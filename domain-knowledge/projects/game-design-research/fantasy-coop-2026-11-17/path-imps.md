## Imps, version F3 (failed by the critic in round K1; kept for the record)

**Fantasy (Claude, changed at this step):** we pick the witch's orchard at night, an imp rides on each of our backs, **it wants something from one of my friends, and only a friend can slap it off**.
One-player test: with one picker there is nobody to want something from and nobody to slap ✅.
Does one player want and fear the same thing? No.
Premise, searched: Cursed Companions (co-op, released November 2025, positive reviews) deals every player a forbidden word that hurts them when anyone says it. Curses! (2001) is a party board game of dealt curses with a winner. **Different because** the curse sits on the body, names a friend, and a friend's hand ends it; nothing here is voice.

**Feelings, each about a friend**
| Feeling | Games that cause it between friends | The players are | What forces the moment there | Does this idea have that? |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Dropped at the feelings step: "Is mine big? Tell me", which had no game behind it. Friends still see the size; no feeling leans on it.

**The rules, one sentence each**
1. Every picker carries one imp all night; it whispers one wish to its carrier, and every wish names a friend.
2. While the wish isn't met the imp grows; while it is met the imp stays the size it is.
3. A big imp takes the legs: it walks the carrier around, tips their basket into the pond, and keeps riding until a friend slaps it.
4. A friend's slap knocks it off; it climbs back small with a new wish from the list, and the slap spills part of the carrier's basket.
5. The carrier hears the wish and can't see the size; friends see the size and can't hear the wish.
6. Each night's deal holds at least one pair of wishes that can't both be met.
Job: fill the cart before dawn.

The list, to give the idea (about fifteen in all, every one costing the named friend something):
"stay within arm's reach of Ana", "Ben must never hold more fruit than you", "Cy carries your basket", "pick only from the tree Ben is picking", "never stand nearer the cart than Ana", "keep your back turned to Cy".

**The choice:** Ben's imp wants me to carry his basket. Do I take it and pick one-handed, or slap the imp off him, spill his fruit and let it come back wanting something else?
**What Ben decides in the same second:** go along with his imp and hand me the basket, or refuse it and turn his back to me for the slap.
**What Cy decides:** his own imp wants him within reach of Ben, so wherever Ben goes for the slap, Cy has to follow or let his own imp grow.

**Scene, 3 friends**
- Ana's imp: "never stand nearer the cart than Ben". Ben's: "never stand nearer the cart than Ana". Cy's: "Ben carries your basket".
- Ana's and Ben's can't both be met: whoever walks fruit to the cart alone grows their own imp.
- Cy says his out loud. Ben takes Cy's basket and now holds two, and has to get them to the cart.
- Ben walks. Ana watches his imp swell and has to choose: leave her tree and walk beside him step for step, or let him go and slap it off him at the cart.
- She slaps. A third of both baskets goes over the grass, and the imp climbs back with "stay within arm's reach of Cy".
- Ben can stand at the cart now, so Ana's wish is easy, and Ben and Cy are tied together for the rest of the night. One slap bought that, and nobody knew what it would deal.

**A perfect crew's round:** the deal always holds a pair that can't both be met, so an imp grows and a slap happens every night ✅.
**The plan by night 5:** the crew knows the list and has a habit for each wish ("carry my basket: slap it").
The wishes interlock and every slap deals a new one, so no card written before the night says who does what ✅ (author's mark).
The check as the critics ran it asks two things, and both hold on paper: something dealt changes the best plan, and each friend needs to hear something they couldn't know before the night.
