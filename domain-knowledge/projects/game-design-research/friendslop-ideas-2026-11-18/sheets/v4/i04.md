# Proposal for the critic: Moonsick

**Fantasy:** It is full moon in a mansion with friends, and each of us fears turning into a werewolf that will hurt the others; we keep each other human until dawn, and it is still me in there if I turn.
One-player test: Alone nobody picks what I find, nobody holds me back from turning, and nobody brings me back; the line doesn't work with one player.

**Players:** 3–6, one crew. **A session:** one night is about 10 minutes; a run is night after night until a mark is missed; a sitting holds 3–6 nights.

**Feelings the game is known for** (not played yet; the feelings aimed at)
"It's up to my shoulders. There's one more, three steps, come on."
"Hold me, hold me, I'm nearly gone." "If I hold you I'm the one who goes."
"BEAM! Beam coming, get out of it!"
"MOVE, MOVE, I can't stop!" "Left side, catch me on the left!"

**The rules, one sentence each**
At dawn the crew drinks the silver cure, and it works only if the cauldron has reached the night's mark and every friend is human.
The cure is made from silver blooms, which open only in moonlight, in the courtyard and in rooms the moon shines into.
Moonlight on you grows your fur from none to full in 100 seconds, shade shrinks it at half that speed, and you see it on your own arms.
A bloom shows only within three steps of a friend with at least half fur, and furry hands crush it, so only a friend with less than half fur can pick it.
A friend can hold another friend with both arms: the held friend's fur stops growing, even in a beam, while the holder's grows as usual; the pair walks at half speed and the holder's hands are full.
Bright beams start at moments and places nobody can know, a few seconds apart or minutes apart, then cross the courtyard and the rooms at running speed where everyone can see them, and in a beam fur grows four times as fast.
At full fur you turn: you are the wolf and you play it, but it never stops running; you can only steer.
The wolf runs anywhere moonlight falls and turns back by itself at the edge of the dark; under a cloud it slows to a walk.
The wolf is as fast as a friend at once and twice as fast after 30 seconds, whatever it hits head-on it bites, and it tramples every bloom it runs over.
A bitten friend drops what they carry and gains half a body of fur.
Two friends who grab the wolf from the side or behind and hold it for 5 seconds turn it back at half fur, so it can find blooms but not pick them, and while they hold it they stand in its light and their own fur grows at double speed.
The mark rises every night, and a night that fails ends the run.

**How much there is:** one mansion with a courtyard, no creatures other than the friends, one kind of bloom, no items, no shop.

**Voice:** none in the game; the friends are on a voice app where everyone hears everyone.

**The choices**
I am the furry guide and the next bloom is deep in the light: walk my clean friend to it while my own fur climbs, or turn back with what we have?
My guide is at nine tenths, far from the shade, and a beam is coming: hold them and walk them back at half speed with my hands full, or pick the bloom in front of me and let them run for it?
A beam is crossing toward us: run out of it and leave the bloom, or stand in it four seconds to pick?
Ana has turned: go out into the light to grab her, with my fur at two thirds, or wait for a cloud to slow her while she tramples the beds?
It is two minutes to dawn, the cauldron is at the mark, and Ben is a wolf: all of us go out for him, or lose the night?

**What the other end decides in the same second**
The clean friend follows the guide deeper, or stops and holds them.
The guide lets themselves be held and walked back, or pulls free for one more bloom.
The wolf steers side-on past the two who came out for it, or misjudges and meets one head-on.
The second holder stays on for the full 5 seconds with their own fur climbing, or lets go at nine tenths.

**Scene, 4 friends**
- Ana is at two thirds fur and walks Ben out; blooms light up three steps around her and Ben picks them clean.
- Ana's fur reaches three quarters; she says "one more past the fountain"; Cy shouts from the doorway that a beam is coming across the roses.
- The beam crosses them; Ana goes to nine tenths in two seconds; Ben drops the blooms and holds her, and her fur stops while his keeps climbing.
- They walk for the shade at half speed; Ben passes half fur on the way and can't pick any more tonight; a second beam catches them at the steps and Ben, holding, turns.
- Ben can't stop: he steers away from Ana, runs the long side of the courtyard, and flattens the bed they just picked.
- Cy and Dee come out to grab him; their fur climbs; Ben shouts "left side" and they catch him on the second pass and hold for 5 seconds while a beam drifts toward them.
- Ben is himself at half fur; Ben, Dee and Ana can all find blooms now, and only Cy still has hands clean enough to pick.

**What experienced crews do:** (not played; Claude's guess) one guide and one picker go out, a third friend watches for beams from the doorway, and whoever passes half fur becomes the next guide.
**What changes it from round to round:** when the beams and the clouds come, where the blooms are, and how far the mark makes the guide walk.
**What carries from one round to the next:** the rising mark only.
**Can a new crew lose? Does it get harder?** Yes: a wolf at dawn or a cauldron under the mark ends the run. The mark rises until a guide who turns back early can't find enough blooms.
