# Critic round

A critic that asks for no severe problems fails a shipped hit too, so its fail said nothing about an idea.
Where the domain has a baseline, the critic compares the design to it.
For game designs the baseline is `~/.claude/domain-knowledge/critic-baseline.md`.

## Steps
1. **Read first:** `~/.claude/domain-knowledge/design-anti-patterns.md`, `~/.claude/domain-knowledge/critic-anti-patterns.md`, the baseline, the project's `CLAUDE.md`, its design doc, the researched docs on the subject (listed in `~/.claude/domain-knowledge/shared/INDEX.md`), and the user's pillars and verdicts (tagged "(user)").
2. **Write the proposal** in the task's folder (e.g. `<topic>-thesis.md`):
   - the user's words that started it, quoted;
   - the problem underneath, in 2–4 lines;
   - at least 3 really different answers, one recommended, with real numbers (ranges, formulas), never "more" or "some";
   - one scene with the fewest players (the user's test: 1 human + bots) and one with the most;
   - each pillar it keeps, bends or drops.
3. **Run the critic:** an isolated general-purpose subagent.
   Its brief:
   - check the problem underneath before the numbers, and say first if it's wrong;
   - check item by item against the files in step 1;
   - play both scenes second by second, and work numbers out with a script instead of guessing;
   - name the strategy that always wins, and pass or fail each point of the co-op check (design-anti-patterns C);
   - list pillars dropped or bent without saying so;
   - mark each finding "the baseline has this too" or "only this design", from the baseline's first table; a finding the baseline has too is listed and never severe by itself;
   - check the design for each part in the baseline's second table, and write a missing part as a finding of this design;
   - a breach of the user's limits stays a finding whatever the baseline does;
   - write short output to `critic-<topic>-r<N>.md` in the same folder: a verdict on the problem underneath, then "worse than the baseline, the same, or better" with the reason in one line, then findings marked severe / medium / minor (problem, example, fix direction), then what it couldn't break.
4. **Merge:** a table under the proposal, with each finding and the decision on it.
   Fix each finding or bring it to the user; never set aside a severe problem with the base idea.
   Rerun the critic until no severe finding is left, at most 3 rounds; only a finding of this design alone, a missing baseline part or a breached limit can be severe.
   A paper verdict is for holes, not the pass gate: the pass gate is the user playing a small build.
5. **Log** what the critic caught in `~/.claude/domain-knowledge/critic-anti-patterns.md` (pattern · this case · fix).
   A repeat goes into its old entry with a count (×N).
6. **Show the user** the sketch: short, a small table, and open choices in the project's `questions.md`.
   Build only after their answer.
