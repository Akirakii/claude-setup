# LAW: game design anchors

Standing rules `/design-idea` applies to every mechanic in this game, beside the pillars in
`MEMORY.md`. Direction and open calls for block build and the core loop:
`docs/TODO_block_build_roadmap.md`.

- **Build cost is never a criterion.** Judge a mechanic on fun, decision-density, and fit to
  the design compass.
- **Write each player decision as what the player builds or places, and where;** the
  creatures choose what to do with it. Why: a decision phrased as an order to a creature names
  a verb the game does not have. When: writing "send them", "make it", "the player has X do
  Y"; listing the decisions a mechanic creates.
- **Build a new object's player interface from verbs and visuals the game already has.** List
  them first (walk-in pickup, a floating item, a show-on-aim hint), and show the user any
  interface detail you still had to invent. Why: an invented verb or readout is a taste call
  the user reverses. When: choosing how the player takes from, reads, or sees the reach of a
  new object; writing a counter label, a mesh stack, or an always-on ring; moving an item or a
  payment between a creature and an object (it walks there and does it on screen, never a
  remote counter change); removing or changing a verb on an object (the prompt, swing and ring
  that offer it are part of the verb).
