# Camping on the fix: patterns against a crew that carries every answer and waits

The problem: a need shows on one player and the others supply the matching fix.
When every fix is small, visible and carryable, the crew carries all of them and waits, and no need is ever hard.
Something has to stop the fix being ready before the need shows, without making the search the challenge.
Tags: [source] = said by the linked source; [read] = Claude's reading of it; [memory] = Claude's memory, not checked; [opinion] = Claude's own.

## Patterns
1. **The cure is a fixed place, not a carried thing.** Overcooked: fixed kitchen stations, and "more tasks than chefs
   available" so nobody can stay at one station [source: Wikipedia, citing Duncan's 2016 Gamasutra deep dive].
   Hospital games send each patient through fixed rooms, which makes the queue the bottleneck [source: PC Gamer,
   Two Point Hospital review].
   Good at: nothing can be brought ahead. Fails: on its own one helper can walk the patient around.
2. **More needs than people.** Overcooked's "more tasks than chefs" and mid-level layout changes that force talking
   [source: Wikipedia; Game Maker's Toolkit].
   Good at: needs the whole crew. Fails against camping: needs that come to the crowd are met by the crowd.
3. **Fixes spoil.** A fix stops working some time after it's picked up [opinion, from memory: burning food in
   Overcooked, spoiling food in Don't Starve; not checked].
   Good at: stops carrying ahead. Fails: not common sense for sunglasses or a cone; a hidden timer.
4. **Split information.** Keep Talking: the defuser alone sees the bomb, the experts alone have the manual; "the fun
   really lives between players" [source: Game Developer on Ben Kane's GDC talk].
   Fails against camping: once the player with the need says it aloud, someone hands the fix over.
5. **Doing the cure is the hard part.** R.E.P.O.: fragile physics loot, moving a piano through a mansion together
   [source: PC Gamer; GamesRadar]. Surgeon Simulator: clumsy hands on purpose, and reviewers say the comedy turns
   into frustration by the tenth try [source: GameSpot].
   Fails against camping: the crowd still stands there with everything.
6. **Someone among you may lie.** Among Us: sabotage pulls the crew apart so someone is alone [source: Android
   Central guide]. Fails: step 1 cut lying, and why anyone would lie is still open (questions.md 17).
