# Report: games similar to Moonsick (2026-11-16)

Full research with sources: `similar-games-research.md`. Nothing in the game was changed.

## Summary
- The biggest recent hits are co-op games where friends face a threat together over proximity voice:
  Lethal Company (240k peak, 10M+ copies), R.E.P.O. (271k peak, 18.5M copies in 2025), Peak (10M copies in ~2 months).
- Betrayal and 1-vs-many games that needed strangers to fill lobbies died small: Deceit 2 (731 peak), Dread Hunger
  (shut down 2024), Evolve (27k to under 7k in a month). Dead by Daylight lasts, but with ten years of a live team.
- FFA physics brawlers sell less and fade faster than co-op: Gang Beasts ~1.3M vs Human: Fall Flat 40M+; Fall Guys
  halved in a month.
- Low retention is normal: friendslop keeps ~3% of players after 30 days and sells in waves (sales, updates, streamers).

## The formula
Friends you already have, proximity voice, a funny premise over polish, $5–20, clip moments, short rounds, a
friend's PC as the host.

## Recommendations

| # | Change | Backed by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Make the hunt the main event, the race a ~90 s–2 min build-up** | Lethal Company, R.E.P.O., Peak; Fall Guys and Party Animals fade |
| 2 | **Proximity voice on by default** | Phasmophobia, Lethal Company, Goose Goose Duck |
| 3 | **Friends-only lobbies on a friend's PC, bots fill; no matchmaking** | Deceit 2, Dread Hunger, Evolve died without it |
| 4 | **Rounds under ~6 min, instant restart** | Among Us, Murder Mystery 2 |
| 5 | Give eaten players something to do (Claude's idea) | Among Us ghosts (from memory) |
| 6 | Tune for 4–6 players, allow 8 | Lethal Company, Peak, R.E.P.O. |
| 7 | $7.99–9.99, plus a free launch day or a deep sale with an update | Content Warning, Peak, Among Us |
| 8 | Keep the werewolf on human moves, no separate levelling | Evolve |
| 9 | Cosmetic-only shop, no deep progression | AppMagic retention data |
| 10 | Clip-friendly big moments (turn, eat, potion throw) | Lethal Company, Content Warning |

Evidence tags: 2, 3, 7, 8, 9 come straight from sources; 1, 4, 6 are Claude's reading across sources; 5 and 10 are Claude's opinion.
