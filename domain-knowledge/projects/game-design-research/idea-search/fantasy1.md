## Passed (5): helping a friend passes the harm on to the one who helped
1. Werewolf Pack: pinning a friend who is turning gets you scratched, so who's at risk changes every round.
2. Vampire Family, tainted blood: a bite feeds you, drains your friend, and passes on whatever is in your blood.
3. Coven: the curse names which witch must bleed, and bleeding weakens her for the next curse.
4. Plague Doctors (weak): treating a friend exposes you; the patient mostly just waits.
5. Possession (weak): you survive by taking a friend's body; unfinished business needs a task list (content).

## Findings
- **Every fantasy passed the force check** (one weak: Possession, Sleepover Monster). A growing pressure plus
  "only a friend relieves it" is easy to write into a fantasy.
- **11 of 16 stopped at "no fixed plan".** When the help just moves a resource (air, blood, heat, light,
  power) or rescues someone, the crew settles into "rotate", "share evenly" or "nearest helps".
- **Survivors: Werewolf Pack, Vampire Family with tainted blood (added on the user's ask), Coven, Plague Doctors (weak), Possession (weak).** What they share: **the help
  moves the harm onto the helper** (pinning gets you scratched, bleeding weakens you, treating exposes you).
  So each round's situation comes from the crew's own choices, and a one-line plan only half works.
- **Bonus:** a pressure that grows on its own also fixes "last one standing": a lone survivor always reaches
  an ending (they lose), so they're never stuck.
- **Improve (no new search; existing research in `../loop1/research/` agrees):** aim the next list at
  "the help moves the harm onto the helper", plus something dealt each round (which harm, on whom).
- **Vampire Family, two versions:** the plain one (#2) moves hunger around, so "feed in a ring" wins. The
  tainted-blood one (#17) also spreads whatever is in your blood, so the help can hurt, and it survives.
