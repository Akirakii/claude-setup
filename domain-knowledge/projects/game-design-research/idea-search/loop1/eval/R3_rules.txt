R301 Possum Toss: Any possum can go limp, and a limp possum can be picked up and thrown by a teammate. The thrown one picks the moment it comes alive in the air.
R302 Hooked on Friends: Your grapple hook sticks only to teammates, and when it lands, whichever of you is braced stays put and the other one gets reeled in.
R303 Megalith Haul: A huge stone slides only over logs, and each log that rolls out behind it must be carried round and laid in front before the stone reaches bare ground.
R304 Push-Off: With dead jetpacks, two crew members who both press kick off each other and fly apart at the same speed in opposite directions, and nothing else changes your course.
R305 Counterweight Shaft: Each rope hangs over a pulley with a player on each end, so the rope you haul in comes out of your partner's side and you rise exactly as far as they drop.
R306 Bedtime Bounce: Everyone bounces on one big bed. If you land right after a teammate lands next to you, you shoot up twice as high and their bounce dies.
R307 Slap Relay: Slap a teammate's hand and they take all your speed on top of their own, while you stop dead.
R308 Strap Grab: Grab a teammate's pack strap and they jerk to a dead stop, unless in that same instant they shrug the pack off. Then the pack and its loot stay in your hands, and they run on lighter.
