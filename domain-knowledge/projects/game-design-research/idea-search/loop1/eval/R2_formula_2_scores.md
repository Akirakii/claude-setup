- **Noise-hunter frame fails R every time (R207, R208, R218, R220).** A threat that goes to the last noise or the loudest voice is steered by one line: decoy, then go quiet. Four of 15 ideas share this frame, and R208, R214 and R216 share one push-apart core, so the batch has fewer distinct cores than IDs.
- **"I act, you choose how it resolves in a second" passes P1–P3, then sags at P4 (R202, R203, R212, R216).** The target's answer is read off visible state (a crack, a floor color), so it becomes a lookup. With public info, nothing private is left to say by round 20 (R4 weak), and three of the four fail R.
- **Only split views or a traitor keep R alive.** R205 (saboteur) is the only R ✓, and it fails F5 because one note at a time idles the crew. The ~ rows (R202, R206, R214, R222) all hide info by position but give no reason to hide (F6 ~), and their pushback is "drifts where you went" (steered by "vary"). Four rows carry lag risk because both players must press at the same moment.
