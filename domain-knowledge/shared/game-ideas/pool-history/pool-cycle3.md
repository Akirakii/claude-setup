# Idea pool (living list; skill `generate-ideas`)

Every line is [job] [choice] [goal]. Claude writes and scores, so it isn't blind. Checks: the skill's `checks.md`.
Marks: ✓ pass · ~ weak · ✗ fail. Earlier versions of the pool: `pool-history/`. Older lines: `passing-lines.md`,
`fantasy1–3.md`.

**Choice shapes** (tag in the Shape column; added cycle 2, Claude):
- **P, push or pass:** keep the harm to make progress, or pass it to a friend / take it from one.
- **T, two ways to help:** two ways to save a friend, each costing something different. The usual form: take it
  onto yourself, or hurt them to end it.
- **U, use a friend:** use a friend's body or skill and leave them worse off.
- **S, swap harms:** cure a friend's harm by giving them a different one.
- **W, where to put it:** choose which part of a friend (or of yourself) takes the harm, and so which ability is lost.

**Look** (added cycle 2, Claude): what the screen shows. It must show the state the choice depends on.

## Rules (the rule step: one sentence plus at most 2 extra rules, then the critic, checks 4.6)
- **Sin Eaters** (c2). Rule: each worm adds one dealt sin to your stain (Wrath: you swing at whoever is nearest;
  Greed: you can't let go of what you hold; Sloth: you move at half speed); a friend pressing bread to you draws half
  of each sin into themselves. Extra: the coffin drags in anyone whose stain is full; two friends can pull them back out.
  - Critic: a 3-line card, "call the sin; bread Greed and Sloth at once; for Wrath, back off and bread from behind when
    it's nearly full." Half-draws mix sins, so the right drawer depends on what each already carries. Low feature
    coupling ✓; 4.6 ~ (the card bends).
- **Coven** (c2). Rule: each lit candle deals one curse that names one witch (rats: a swarm walks at her and slows
  whoever it touches; fire: a ring closes on her); a cut from another witch's knife on the named witch's palm ends the
  curse at once but halves her speed until the next candle. Extra: a witch caught by the fire ring is out.
  - Critic: a 2-line card, "cut on fire; on rats, run." The next candle may name the slowed witch again. ✓; 4.6 ~.
- **Grave Robbers** (c2). Rule: opening a coffin wakes its corpse, which grabs the nearest robber's leg and drags them
  toward the grave; pulling for 3 seconds frees them and the corpse grabs the puller instead; slamming the lid frees
  them at once and their rings drop back into the coffin. Extra: the watchman's lantern circles the churchyard and
  anyone in its light must freeze.
  - Critic: a 1-line card, "slam whoever holds the fewest rings." Pull chains, the lantern freezing a puller, and
    corpses that turn out weak or strong bend it. ✓; 4.6 ~.
- **Werewolf Pack** (c3). Rule: standing in the moonbeam grows fur on you; at full fur you turn, and your wolf is
  pulled toward the nearest friend and swipes on its own while you fight the pull; two friends holding you for
  3 seconds turn you back, and each swipe that lands moves some fur onto the one it hits. Extra: the cure needs
  a set number of stirs in the beam.
  - Critic: a 2-line card, "one stirs at a time and swaps out when furry; everyone piles on whoever turns." Swipes
    spread fur unevenly, so after a pile-on the next stirrer may already be half-furred, and a turned friend fighting
    the pull makes pile-ons miss. ✓; 4.6 ~ (bends).
- **Midas Thieves** (c3). Rule: every crate you touch turns to gold and adds gold to your arm; gripping a friend for
  2 seconds moves your gold into the part you grip (hand: can't pick up; foot: half speed; mouth: muted on voice
  chat) until the ferry lands. Extra: gold that reaches your shoulder makes you a statue (out).
  - Critic: a 1-line card, "always put it in the feet." Nothing in the rule makes walking matter more than holding
    or talking, so it may hold. ✓; 4.6 ~ leaning ✗. Next fix: something dealt each round makes a different part
    matter most.
- **Hypnotist's Show** (c3, failed). Rule tried: when the word is said, everyone hypnotized acts it out for 5 seconds;
  only the hypnotized can creep toward the watch (the awake are spotted and sent back); clapping wakes a friend and
  puts you under. Being under is what you want, so clapping is either free or never worth doing. ✗ (force 0.3, and
  the friend act is optional). Moved to Failed.
